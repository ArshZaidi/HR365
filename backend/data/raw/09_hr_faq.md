## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Onboarding FAQs

**Q: What do I need to bring on day 1?**
A: ID proof, educational certificates, previous employment documents, and passport-size photographs. See HR365-POL-001.

**Q: When does my probation end?**
A: 90 calendar days from your date of joining. Confirmation is communicated within 10 working days after. See HR365-POL-001.

**Q: What is a "buddy"?**
A: A peer assigned to help you settle in during your first 90 days. See HR365-POL-001.

**Q: How do I get my ID card?**
A: Collected from the IT desk on day 1. See HR365-POL-001.

### 4.2 Working Hours and Remote Work

**Q: What are the standard working hours?**
A: 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday (9 hours including lunch). See HR365-POL-002.

**Q: What are core collaboration hours?**
A: 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM — all employees must be reachable. See HR365-POL-002.

**Q: Can I work from home?**
A: Yes, if your role is eligible. Ad-hoc WFH requires 1 working day advance notice. Recurring hybrid requires manager + HR approval. See HR365-POL-004.

**Q: Do I need VPN for WFH?**
A: Yes, for all access to internal systems. See HR365-POL-008.

**Q: Can I WFH from another city?**
A: Not without prior HR approval for tax and compliance reasons. See HR365-POL-004.

### 4.3 Attendance FAQs

**Q: What counts as a full day?**
A: 8 hours of logged time within 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM. See HR365-POL-002.

**Q: What time is considered late?**
A: Check-in after 9:45 AM. 3 late marks in a month = 0.5-day CL deduction. See HR365-POL-002.

**Q: How do I correct an attendance record?**
A: Raise a correction request via ESS within 5 working days. Manager approval required. See HR365-POL-002.

**Q: What if I forget to check out?**
A: System assigns 6:30 PM. Correct via ESS if the actual time differed. See HR365-POL-002.

**Q: Can I mark attendance for a colleague?**
A: No. This is fraud and a Code of Conduct violation. See HR365-POL-005.

### 4.4 Leave FAQs

**Q: How do I apply for leave?**
A: Submit via the HR365 portal at least 2 working days in advance for planned leave. See HR365-POL-003.

**Q: Where do I see my leave status?**
A: The "My Leave" tab on the ESS portal; updates in real time. See HR365-POL-003.

**Q: Can I cancel approved leave?**
A: Yes, up to 1 day before the start date via the portal; later cancellations need manager approval. See HR365-POL-003.

**Q: How many leave days do I get per year?**
A: Confirmed employees: 12 CL + 10 SL + 15 EL = 37 days. Additional leave for ML, PL, BL. See HR365-POL-003.

**Q: Can I take leave during probation?**
A: Only SL and BL. CL and EL become available on confirmation. See HR365-POL-003.

**Q: Do I need a medical certificate for sick leave?**
A: Only for 3 or more consecutive days. Upload within 5 working days of return. See HR365-POL-003.

### 4.5 Payroll FAQs

**Q: When is salary credited?**
A: Last working day of each month. See HR365-POL-007.

**Q: Where is my payslip?**
A: Published on the HR365 Finance portal by the 2nd working day of the following month. See HR365-POL-007.

**Q: What if my salary is incorrect?**
A: Report within 7 working days of payslip publication via the Finance Helpdesk. See HR365-POL-007.

**Q: When do I get Form 16?**
A: By June 15 each year for the preceding financial year. See HR365-POL-007.

**Q: What is the payroll cutoff?**
A: The 25th of each month. Changes after that apply to the next cycle. See HR365-POL-007.

### 4.6 Benefits FAQs

**Q: When does health insurance start?**
A: Day 1 of joining. See HR365-POL-006.

**Q: Can I add my parents to insurance?**
A: Yes, up to 4 dependents (spouse, children, parents, parents-in-law). See HR365-POL-006.

**Q: How do I claim internet reimbursement?**
A: Submit the bill via the HR365 Finance portal each month, up to ₹1,000. See HR365-POL-006.

**Q: What is the L&D allowance?**
A: Up to ₹20,000/year for role-relevant courses. See HR365-POL-006.

**Q: How long do I have to submit a reimbursement?**
A: Within 60 days of the expense date. See HR365-POL-006.

### 4.7 IT FAQs

**Q: When do I need VPN?**
A: Whenever accessing internal systems remotely, including over public Wi-Fi. See HR365-POL-008.

**Q: How do I report a lost laptop?**
A: Contact it-security@nimbusworks.example or the 24×7 IT hotline within 2 hours. See HR365-POL-008.

**Q: Can I use my personal phone for work email?**
A: No. Use company devices only. See HR365-POL-008.

**Q: How often must I change my password?**
A: Every 90 days. Minimum 12 characters. See HR365-POL-008.

### 4.8 HR-Contact FAQs

**Q: How do I contact HR?**
A: Raise a ticket on the HR365 HR Helpdesk portal, email hr@nimbusworks.example, or call extension 4001.

**Q: How do I raise an HR ticket?**
A: Use the "Raise a Ticket" option on the HR365 portal home screen; acknowledged within 2 working days.

**Q: What if my query isn't resolved?**
A: Escalate to the HR Head via hrhead@nimbusworks.example.

### 4.9 Conduct and Grievance FAQs

**Q: How do I report harassment?**
A: Via the HR365 Grievance module, directly with HR, or with the Internal Committee. See HR365-POL-010.

**Q: Is there an anonymous reporting channel?**
A: Yes — the Ethics Hotline, accessible via the HR365 portal. See HR365-POL-005.

**Q: Can I be retaliated against for reporting?**
A: No. Retaliation is prohibited and grounds for disciplinary action. See HR365-POL-005.

### 4.10 Separation FAQs

**Q: How long is the notice period?**
A: Per your employment agreement (typically 30–90 days). See HR365-POL-001.

**Q: When is final settlement processed?**
A: Within 45 days of the last working day. See HR365-POL-007.

**Q: Can I take leave during notice period?**
A: Only with HR approval in addition to manager approval. See HR365-POL-003.

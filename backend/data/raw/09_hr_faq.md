## Purpose
Provides short, quick-reference answers to common employee questions. This FAQ is a reference document and does not override the authoritative policies it summarizes. Where a conflict exists, the applicable source policy is authoritative.

## Scope
Applies to all employees as a first point of reference for routine questions.

## Policy

### Leave FAQs
- **How do I apply for leave?** Submit a request via the HR365 portal at least 2 working days in advance (see Leave Policy, HR365-POL-003).
- **Where do I see my leave status?** Check the "My Leave" tab on the HR365 Employee Self-Service portal; status updates in real time.
- **Can I cancel approved leave?** Yes, up to 1 day before the start date via the portal; later cancellations need manager approval.

### Attendance FAQs
- **What happens if I arrive late?** Check-in after 9:45 AM is marked "Late"; 3 late marks in a month convert to a 0.5-day leave deduction (see Attendance Policy, HR365-POL-002).
- **How do I correct an attendance record?** Submit a correction request within 5 working days via the HR365 portal, with manager approval required.

### Payroll FAQs
- **When is salary credited?** On the last working day of each month (see Payroll Policy, HR365-POL-007).
- **Where is my payslip?** Published on the HR365 Finance portal by the 2nd working day of the following month.
- **What if my salary is incorrect?** Report it within 7 working days of payslip publication via the Finance Helpdesk.

### IT FAQs
- **When do I need VPN?** Whenever accessing internal systems remotely, including over public Wi-Fi (see IT Security Policy, HR365-POL-008).
- **How do I report a lost laptop?** Contact it-security@nimbusworks.example or the 24x7 IT hotline within 2 hours of noticing the loss.

### HR-Contact FAQs
- **How do I contact HR?** Raise a ticket on the HR365 HR Helpdesk portal, email hr@nimbusworks.example, or call extension 4001.
- **How do I raise an HR ticket?** Use the "Raise a Ticket" option on the HR365 portal home screen; tickets are acknowledged within 2 working days.

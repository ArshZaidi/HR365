## Purpose
Sets the standards of professional behavior, integrity, and conduct expected of all NimbusWorks employees, and defines how conduct concerns are reported and handled.

## Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and interns, in the workplace, at company events, and in official remote/virtual interactions.

## Policy

### 1. Professional Behaviour
Employees must conduct themselves with honesty, courtesy, and professionalism in all interactions with colleagues, clients, vendors, and the public.

### 2. Respect and Inclusion
- NimbusWorks maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward discrimination or harassment based on gender, religion, caste, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic.
- All employees must complete an annual Respect at Workplace training module.

### 3. Conflicts of Interest
- Employees must disclose any outside business interest, family relationship with a vendor/client, or other situation that could reasonably create a conflict of interest, via the HR365 Conflict Disclosure form.
- Undisclosed conflicts discovered later may result in disciplinary action.

### 4. Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential company, client, or employee information to unauthorized parties, during or after employment, except as required by law.

### 5. Misconduct Reporting
- Misconduct (harassment, discrimination, fraud, policy violation) may be reported via the HR365 Grievance module, in person to HR, or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline.
- Reports are acknowledged within 2 working days and investigated per the Workplace Safety and Grievance Policy (HR365-POL-010).

### 6. Non-Retaliation
NimbusWorks prohibits retaliation against any employee who reports misconduct in good faith or participates in an investigation. Retaliation is itself grounds for disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## Exceptions
None. This policy applies uniformly; no role-based exceptions are permitted.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: uphold this code and complete required training.
- Managers: model expected behavior and escalate reported concerns promptly.
- HR Department: investigate reports impartially and maintain confidentiality of the process.

## Contact / Escalation
Conduct concerns: conduct@nimbusworks.example or the anonymous Ethics Hotline (see HR365 portal). Escalation: HR Head, hrhead@nimbusworks.example.

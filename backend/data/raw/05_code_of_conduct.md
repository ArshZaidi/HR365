## 1. Purpose

Sets the standards of professional behavior, integrity, and conduct expected of all NimbusWorks employees, and defines how conduct concerns are reported and handled.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, and interns:

- In the workplace
- At company-sponsored events (on or off premises)
- In all official remote/virtual interactions
- In any setting where the employee represents NimbusWorks

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Misconduct | Any action violating this code or Company policy |
| Harassment | Unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic, or that creates a hostile environment |
| Discrimination | Unequal treatment based on a protected characteristic |
| Retaliation | Adverse action against a reporter or witness |
| Conflict of Interest | Situation where personal interests may conflict with Company interests |
| Confidential Information | Non-public information belonging to the Company, clients, or employees |
| Internal Committee (IC) | Committee constituted for harassment matters per applicable law |
| Ethics Hotline | Anonymous reporting channel managed independently |
| Need-to-Know | Principle of limiting information access to those who require it for their role |

## 4. Professional Behaviour

Employees must conduct themselves with honesty, courtesy, and professionalism in all interactions with colleagues, clients, vendors, and the public.

Expected conduct includes:

- Respectful and constructive communication
- Punctuality and reliability
- Accountability for commitments
- Collaboration over individual agendas
- Respect for shared workspaces and facilities

## 5. Respect and Inclusion

NimbusWorks maintains a **zero-tolerance policy** toward discrimination or harassment based on:

- Gender
- Religion
- Caste
- Disability
- Age
- Sexual orientation
- National origin
- Marital status
- Any other protected characteristic

All employees must complete an annual **Respect at Workplace** training module. Non-completion within the stated window is escalated.

## 6. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any situation that could reasonably create a conflict of interest, via the HR365 **Conflict Disclosure form**, including:

- Outside business interests that compete or intersect with NimbusWorks
- Family or close personal relationships with vendors, clients, or colleagues in overlapping reporting lines
- Personal investments in entities doing business with the Company
- Board memberships or advisory roles outside the Company

Undisclosed conflicts discovered later may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## 7. Confidentiality

Employees must not disclose confidential Company, client, or employee information to unauthorized parties:

- During employment
- After employment ends
- Except as required by law

Scope of confidential information includes:

- Financial, strategic, and operational data
- Client information and contracts
- Employee personal information
- Trade secrets, source code, product roadmaps
- Intellectual property

Disclosure obligations survive the end of employment.

## 8. Use of Company Resources

Company resources are provided for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted within reason. Prohibited uses include:

- Any illegal activity
- Activities that create liability for the Company
- Excessive personal use interfering with work
- Running a side business
- Political or religious campaigning

## 9. Misconduct Reporting

Misconduct (harassment, discrimination, fraud, policy violation) may be reported via:

- HR365 Grievance module
- In person to HR
- Anonymous Ethics Hotline

Reports are acknowledged within **2 working days** and investigated per HR365-POL-010.

Report contents should include (where available):

- Date, time, and location of incident
- Description of behavior
- Names of individuals involved
- Names of witnesses (if any)
- Any supporting evidence

## 10. Non-Retaliation

NimbusWorks prohibits retaliation against any employee who:

- Reports misconduct in good faith
- Participates in an investigation
- Supports a colleague in raising a concern

Retaliation is itself grounds for disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## 11. Disciplinary Process

Violations of this code are addressed through a graduated process:

1. **Verbal warning** — for minor first-time issues
2. **Written warning** — for repeated or more serious issues
3. **Final written warning** — with a defined improvement window
4. **Suspension** — with or without pay, per severity
5. **Termination** — for gross misconduct or repeated violations

Serious violations may result in immediate termination without prior warnings, including:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Fraud or theft
- Physical violence or threats
- Breach of confidentiality
- Falsification of records
- Attendance fraud
- Retaliation against a reporter

## 12. Specific Conduct Rules

| Rule | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Alcohol and drugs | Not permitted on premises or during work hours |
| Smoking | Only in designated areas |
| Weapons | Prohibited on all Company premises |
| Gift acceptance | Nominal gifts (under ₹2,000) permitted; larger gifts require disclosure |
| Social media | Must not disclose confidential information; must disclose employment when commenting on Company-related matters |
| Political activity | Not permitted during work hours or using Company resources |
| Personal relationships | Must be disclosed if a reporting line is affected |

## 14. Responsibilities

**Employees:** Uphold this code, complete required training, disclose conflicts.
**Managers:** Model expected behavior, escalate reported concerns promptly, do not investigate independently.
**HR:** Investigate reports impartially, maintain confidentiality, maintain records for 7 years.
**Internal Committee:** Handle harassment complaints per applicable law.

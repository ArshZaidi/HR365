## Purpose
This handbook introduces NimbusWorks Pvt. Ltd. ("the Company") to new and existing employees and sets out the general rules, expectations, and administrative processes that apply across all departments.

## Scope
Applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract employees of NimbusWorks, across all office locations and remote work arrangements.

## Policy

### 1. Company Overview and Departments
NimbusWorks is organized into five departments: Engineering, Product, Sales & Marketing, Finance & Operations, and Human Resources. Each department is led by a Department Head who reports to the CEO.

### 2. Employment, Probation, and Onboarding
- New hires undergo a probation period of 90 calendar days from their date of joining.
- During probation, employment may be terminated by either party with 7 days' written notice.
- Confirmation of employment is communicated in writing by HR within 10 working days after probation ends.
- Onboarding includes: ID card issuance, IT account setup, benefits enrollment, and a mandatory orientation session within the first week.

### 3. Working Hours and Breaks
- Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday (9 hours including a 1-hour lunch break).
- Two additional short breaks of 15 minutes each are permitted, one in the morning and one in the afternoon.
- Core collaboration hours (when all employees must be reachable) are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

### 4. Professional Expectations
- Employees are expected to act with honesty, punctuality, and respect toward colleagues, clients, and vendors.
- Dress code is "business casual" on regular days and "formal" for client-facing meetings.
- Use of company resources (email, laptop, software) is for business purposes; incidental personal use is permitted within reason.

### 5. Updating Employee Information
Employees must update personal details (address, phone number, emergency contact, bank details) via the HR365 Employee Self-Service portal within 15 days of any change. HR does not update records based on verbal requests alone.

### 6. How to Contact HR
- General queries: raise a ticket via the HR365 HR Helpdesk portal.
- Urgent matters: email hr@nimbusworks.example or call the HR Helpdesk at extension 4001.
- Walk-in support is available at the HR desk, 10:00 AM–5:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

## Exceptions
Department-specific working hours (e.g., shift-based Support roles) are governed by the Attendance Policy (HR365-POL-002) and override the standard hours listed above where applicable.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: comply with this handbook and keep personal records current.
- Managers: ensure new hires complete onboarding within the first week.
- HR Department: maintain and communicate policy updates.

## Contact / Escalation
HR Department — hr@nimbusworks.example / extension 4001. Unresolved queries may be escalated to the HR Head via hrhead@nimbusworks.example.

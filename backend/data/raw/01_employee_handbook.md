## 1. Purpose

This handbook introduces NimbusWorks Pvt. Ltd. ("the Company") to new and existing employees and sets out the general rules, expectations, and administrative processes that apply across all departments and locations.

It is the **entry point** to the Company's policy framework. Where a specific topic has a dedicated policy, this handbook provides an overview and links to the authoritative document. Where no dedicated policy exists, this handbook is authoritative.

This handbook does not create a contract of employment. Employment terms are governed by the individual employment agreement and applicable law.

---

## 2. Scope

This handbook applies to:

- All full-time employees
- All part-time employees
- All contract employees on engagements exceeding 90 calendar days
- All interns (with additional rules per HR365-POL-012)
- All office locations of NimbusWorks
- All remote and hybrid work arrangements

It does not apply to independent consultants or vendors, whose engagement is governed by their agreement.

## 4. Policy Statement

NimbusWorks is committed to:

- Providing a safe, inclusive, and professional workplace
- Treating all employees with dignity and respect
- Clear, transparent, and consistently applied rules
- Fair and timely resolution of employee concerns
- Supporting employee growth and wellbeing

Employees are expected to:

- Perform their duties with honesty, diligence, and professionalism
- Respect colleagues, clients, and company property
- Comply with Company policies and applicable law
- Raise concerns promptly through the proper channels
- Maintain confidentiality of Company and employee information

This handbook is not a substitute for reading the underlying policies. Employees are responsible for knowing and complying with all applicable policies.

## 7. Probation and Onboarding

### 7.1 Probation Period

- **Duration:** 90 calendar days from date of joining
- **Extensions:** Up to 30 days, only with written notice and documented reason
- **Termination during probation:** Either party may terminate with **7 days' written notice**
- **Confirmation:** Communicated in writing by HR within **10 working days** after probation ends

### 7.2 Confirmation

Confirmation is based on:

- Performance during probation (as assessed by the manager)
- Compliance with Company policies
- Completion of required onboarding training
- No active disciplinary action

### 7.3 Onboarding Checklist

New hires must complete the following within the first week:

1. **ID card issuance** — collected from the IT desk on day 1
2. **IT account setup** — email, HR365 portal, VPN, and system access
3. **Benefits enrollment** — health insurance and other benefits per HR365-POL-006
4. **Mandatory orientation session** — HR-led, within the first week
5. **Policy acknowledgment** — sign-off on this handbook and related policies via the portal
6. **Respect at Workplace training** — completed within 30 days (see HR365-POL-005)
7. **IT Security briefing** — MFA setup and security awareness (see HR365-POL-008)
8. **Buddy assignment** — an experienced team member assigned as a point of contact

### 7.4 Buddy Program

Every new hire is assigned a "buddy" — a peer in the same or adjacent team. The buddy's role:

- Answer day-to-day questions
- Introduce the team
- Help navigate internal tools
- Provide informal support during the first 90 days

Buddies are not managers and are not responsible for the new hire's performance.

### 7.5 Probation Leave Restrictions

During probation, employees are eligible only for:

- Sick Leave (per HR365-POL-003)
- Bereavement Leave (per HR365-POL-003)

Other leave types become available on confirmation.

## 8. Working Hours and Breaks

### 8.1 Standard Hours

- **Shift:** 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday
- **Total:** 9 hours including a 1-hour lunch break
- **Actual work hours:** 8 hours per day

### 8.2 Core Collaboration Hours

**11:00 AM – 4:00 PM** — all employees must be reachable and available for calls and meetings.

### 8.3 Breaks

- **Lunch:** 1 hour (flexible within 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM)
- **Short breaks:** Two 15-minute breaks, one morning and one afternoon
- Breaks are within standard shift hours and cannot be combined to leave early

### 8.4 Rotational Shifts

Roles requiring 24×7 or extended-hours coverage (Support, Operations) follow published shift rosters. Shift rules per HR365-POL-002, §10.

### 8.5 Overtime

NimbusWorks does not formally operate an overtime system for most roles. Occasional extended work is expected as part of the role. Pre-approved weekend or holiday work earns Comp-Off per HR365-POL-003, §7.7.

### 8.6 Flex Time

Employees may request flexibility in start and end times with manager approval, provided:

- Core hours are maintained
- Total daily logged time meets the standard
- Business needs are not affected

## 9. Professional Expectations

### 9.1 General Conduct

Employees are expected to act with:

- **Honesty** — truthful in communication, records, and reports
- **Punctuality** — arriving on time for work, meetings, and commitments
- **Respect** — toward colleagues, clients, vendors, and visitors
- **Integrity** — declining to engage in unethical practices
- **Accountability** — owning outcomes and learning from mistakes
- **Collaboration** — supporting team goals over individual agendas

### 9.2 Communication Norms

- **Responsiveness:** Reply to messages within 4 hours during working hours
- **Channels:** Use company-approved tools (email, chat) for all work communication
- **Meetings:** Arrive on time; cancel with reasonable notice if needed
- **Documentation:** Record important decisions in writing
- **Escalation:** Raise issues early via the manager

### 9.3 Dress Code

- **Regular days:** Business casual (collared shirt, chinos, kurtas, sarees, or equivalent)
- **Client-facing meetings:** Formal attire
- **Office events:** As specified in the invite

Employees should dress in a manner that is professional, comfortable, and respectful of the workplace.

### 9.4 Use of Company Resources

Company resources (email, laptop, software, internet, printers) are provided for business purposes.

- Incidental personal use is permitted within reason
- Excessive personal use is not permitted
- Use of company resources for illegal or unethical activity is prohibited
- Company email may be monitored per the IT Security Policy (HR365-POL-008)

### 9.5 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any situation that could create a conflict of interest, including:

- Outside business interests that compete or intersect with NimbusWorks
- Family or close personal relationships with vendors, clients, or other employees (where reporting lines intersect)
- Personal investments in entities doing business with the Company
- Board memberships or advisory roles outside the Company

Disclosures are made via the HR365 Conflict Disclosure form. See HR365-POL-005 for full rules.

### 9.6 Confidentiality

Employees must not disclose:

- Company financial, strategic, or operational information
- Client information
- Employee personal information (unless authorized)
- Trade secrets, source code, or intellectual property

Obligations continue after employment ends. See HR365-POL-005, §4.

## 11. Updating Employee Information

### 11.1 Self-Service Portal

Employees must update personal details via the HR365 ESS portal within **15 days** of any change. HR does not update records based on verbal requests alone.

Details to keep current:

- Residential address
- Personal phone number
- Emergency contact(s)
- Bank account details
- Nominee for insurance and PF
- Marital status (for benefits updates)
- Educational qualifications (for certain roles)
- Passport details (for travel roles)

### 11.2 Verification

Some updates require verification:

- Bank account changes: verified against a cancelled cheque or bank statement
- Address changes: verified against a utility bill or rental agreement (if address is used for tax purposes)
- Nominee changes: requires signed nominee form

### 11.3 Failure to Update

Failure to update records within the stated window may lead to:

- Delay in payroll processing
- Delay in benefits claims
- Missed communications from HR
- Rejection of certain claims (e.g., insurance claims for unlisted dependents)

### 11.4 HR Data Privacy

HR handles employee data per the Company's privacy policy and applicable data protection laws. Employee data is not shared with third parties except as required for payroll, insurance, or legal compliance.

## 19. Separation and Exit

### 19.1 Resignation

- Submit a written resignation via the HR365 portal
- Notice period: as per employment agreement (typically 30–90 days)
- Shortened notice requires manager + HR approval

### 19.2 Termination

The Company may terminate employment:

- During probation: 7 days' written notice (per §7.1)
- After confirmation: per employment agreement and applicable law
- For cause (misconduct): process per HR365-POL-005

### 19.3 Notice Period

During notice period, employees must:

- Continue to perform duties
- Hand over responsibilities systematically
- Not accept outside employment that conflicts with duties
- Return all company property within 5 working days of last working day

### 19.4 Exit Checklist

Before the last working day:

1. Hand over all projects and documentation
2. Return company laptop, monitor, headset, and other equipment
3. Return ID card and access cards
4. Complete exit interview with HR
5. Clear financial obligations (advances, reimbursements)
6. Sign final settlement acknowledgment

### 19.5 Final Settlement

- Processed within **45 days** of the last working day
- Includes salary up to last day, EL encashment (up to 30 days), and any statutory dues
- Deductions for unpaid leave, unreturned equipment, or outstanding advances apply

### 19.6 Post-Employment Obligations

- Confidentiality obligations continue indefinitely
- Non-solicitation and non-compete clauses apply where specified in the employment agreement
- Intellectual property created during employment belongs to the Company

## 22. Frequently Asked Questions

### Onboarding

**Q: What do I need to bring on day 1?**
A: ID proof, educational certificates, previous employment documents (if any), and passport-size photographs. Details are in your welcome email.

**Q: Who is my manager?**
A: Your reporting line is stated in your employment agreement and visible on the HR365 portal under your profile.

**Q: What is a "buddy"?**
A: A peer assigned to help you settle in during your first 90 days. Not a manager, just a friendly point of contact.

**Q: When do I get my ID card?**
A: On day 1 at the IT desk.

**Q: How long is probation?**
A: 90 calendar days from your joining date.

### Working Hours

**Q: Do I have to work exactly 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM?**
A: The standard shift is 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM. You may request flexible timings with manager approval, provided core hours (11:00 AM – 4:00 PM) are maintained.

**Q: Can I take lunch at any time?**
A: Yes, within 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM. Standard is 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM.

**Q: Do I need to be reachable outside working hours?**
A: No. Occasional after-hours communication is not a requirement, unless in an on-call role.

### Communication

**Q: How quickly should I respond to messages?**
A: Within 30 minutes during working hours for chat; 4 hours for email.

**Q: What if I need to work from a different city?**
A: Temporary WFH from another city requires prior HR approval for tax and compliance reasons.

**Q: Can I use my personal phone for work calls?**
A: Yes, for occasional calls. For regular work, use company-provided tools. See HR365-POL-008 for device security.

### Conduct

**Q: What is the dress code?**
A: Business casual on regular days; formal for client meetings.

**Q: Can I accept a gift from a vendor?**
A: Nominal gifts (e.g., under ₹2,000) may be accepted but must be disclosed if the vendor relationship creates a conflict. See HR365-POL-005.

**Q: What if I have a second job?**
A: Outside employment must be disclosed if it could create a conflict of interest. See HR365-POL-005, §3.

**Q: What if I see harassment?**
A: Report via HR, the Internal Committee, or the anonymous Ethics Hotline. See HR365-POL-010.

### Benefits

**Q: When does health insurance start?**
A: From day 1 of joining.

**Q: Can I add my parents to health insurance?**
A: Yes, up to 4 dependents (spouse, children, parents, parents-in-law). Enrollment windows are announced.

**Q: How do I claim internet reimbursement?**
A: Submit the bill via the HR365 Finance portal each month.

### IT and Devices

**Q: What devices does the Company provide?**
A: Laptop. On request (for confirmed remote employees), a monitor and headset.

**Q: Can I install apps on my work laptop?**
A: Only approved apps. Request approval via the HR365 IT Request module.

**Q: What if I lose my laptop?**
A: Report immediately to it-security@nimbusworks.example within 2 hours.

### Separation

**Q: How long is the notice period?**
A: Per your employment agreement (typically 30–90 days).

**Q: When is final settlement processed?**
A: Within 45 days of the last working day.

**Q: Can I take leave during notice period?**
A: Only with HR approval in addition to manager approval. See HR365-POL-003.

**Q: Do I need to return the laptop?**
A: Yes, within 5 working days of the last working day.

## 23. Worked Scenarios

### Scenario 1: New Joiner's First Week

**Day 1:** Collect ID card, complete IT setup, meet manager and buddy, HR orientation.
**Day 2–3:** Shadow team members, complete mandatory training modules.
**Day 4:** First team meeting, assigned initial tasks.
**Day 5:** Check-in with manager, review of week 1.

### Scenario 2: Requesting a Flex Start

**Employee:** Confirmed, personal commitments require a 10:30 AM start.
**Correct approach:** Submit request to manager; if approved, adjust daily schedule to end at 7:30 PM. Core hours maintained.

### Scenario 3: Client Meeting Dress Code

**Event:** Client-facing presentation.
**Dress:** Formal business attire.
**If unsure:** Ask the manager in advance.

### Scenario 4: Reporting a Safety Hazard

**Event:** Employee notices a broken chair in the meeting room.
**Correct approach:** Report via the HR365 Safety Report form or to the Safety Officer. Do not use the broken chair.

### Scenario 5: Conflict of Interest Disclosure

**Event:** Employee's spouse is a vendor's account manager.
**Correct approach:** Disclose via the HR365 Conflict Disclosure form. HR reviews and advises on management.

### Scenario 6: Harassment Report

**Event:** Employee experiences inappropriate comments from a colleague.
**Correct approach:** Report via HR, the Internal Committee, or the Ethics Hotline. Confidentiality is maintained; non-retaliation applies.

### Scenario 7: Grievance Process

**Step 1:** Employee raises grievance via HR365 portal.
**Step 2:** HR conducts fact-finding within 10 working days.
**Step 3:** Findings communicated in writing.
**Step 4:** Employee may request review by HR Head within 5 working days if dissatisfied.

### Scenario 8: Resignation

**Event:** Employee decides to move on.
**Correct approach:** Submit resignation via HR365 portal; serve the full notice period unless shortened by manager + HR; complete exit checklist.

### Scenario 9: Exit Interview

**Event:** HR schedules exit interview.
**Correct approach:** Provide honest feedback about experience, culture, and suggestions. Information is used for improvement and remains confidential where requested.

### Scenario 10: After-Hours Request

**Event:** Manager sends a message at 10:00 PM on a weekday.
**Correct approach:** Not urgent = respond next morning. Urgent = respond immediately if aware. Managers should avoid after-hours messages except in emergencies.

### Scenario 11: Laptop Lost During Travel

**Event:** Employee loses laptop at an airport.
**Correct approach:** Report to it-security@nimbusworks.example within 2 hours. IT may remotely wipe the device. Incident logged.

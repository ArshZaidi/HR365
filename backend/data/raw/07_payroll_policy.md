## Purpose
Describes NimbusWorks' payroll cycle, payslip access, deductions, and the process for resolving salary discrepancies.

## Scope
Applies to all employees on the NimbusWorks payroll, across all employment types.

## Policy

### 1. Payroll Cycle
- Salary is processed monthly and credited on the last working day of each month.
- Attendance and leave records are locked for payroll processing on the 25th of each month; changes after this date apply to the following month's cycle.

### 2. Payslips
- Payslips are published on the HR365 Finance portal by the 2nd working day of the following month.
- Payslips include gross pay, deductions, net pay, and leave balance summary.

### 3. Deductions
Standard deductions include: Provident Fund (12% of basic pay), Professional Tax (as per state slab), and Income Tax (TDS, as per declared tax regime).

### 4. Tax Documents
- Form 16 is issued annually by June 15 for the preceding financial year.
- Employees must submit investment declarations for tax purposes by January 31 each year via the HR365 Finance portal.

### 5. Salary Corrections
- Discrepancies must be reported via the HR365 Finance Helpdesk within 7 working days of payslip publication.
- Verified corrections are processed in the next payroll cycle; urgent cases may be processed via off-cycle payment with Finance Head approval.

### 6. Payroll Escalation
Unresolved salary queries after 7 working days are escalated to the Payroll Manager, and further to the Finance Head if unresolved after an additional 5 working days.

## Exceptions
Employees joining or exiting mid-month receive prorated salary based on actual working days in that month.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: verify payslips and report discrepancies promptly.
- Managers: confirm attendance/leave data accuracy before the monthly lock date.
- Finance Department: process payroll accurately and on schedule.

## Contact / Escalation
Payroll queries: payroll@nimbusworks.example. Escalation: Finance Head, finance-head@nimbusworks.example.

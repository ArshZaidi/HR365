## 1. Purpose

Describes NimbusWorks' payroll cycle, payslip access, deductions, tax documents, and the process for resolving salary discrepancies.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees on the NimbusWorks payroll, across all employment types (full-time, part-time, contract exceeding 90 days).

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Payroll Cycle | The monthly period for which salary is processed |
| Payroll Cutoff | The date (25th) after which attendance/leave changes apply to the next cycle |
| Payslip | Monthly statement of gross pay, deductions, and net pay |
| Gross Pay | Total earnings before deductions |
| Net Pay | Amount credited after all deductions |
| CTC | Cost to Company — total compensation including benefits |
| PF | Provident Fund — statutory retirement contribution |
| TDS | Tax Deducted at Source — income tax withheld monthly |
| Form 16 | Annual tax certificate issued to employees |
| Off-Cycle Payment | Payment outside the regular monthly cycle |
| Final Settlement | Full and final dues processed on exit |

## 4. Payroll Cycle

- Salary is processed **monthly** and credited on the **last working day** of each month
- Attendance and leave records are **locked** on the **25th** of each month
- Changes actioned after the 25th apply to the **following month's** cycle
- If the last working day falls on a holiday or weekend, salary is credited on the preceding working day

## 5. Payslips

- Published on the HR365 Finance portal by the **2nd working day** of the following month
- Contents:
  - Gross pay (basic, HRA, allowances)
  - Deductions (PF, professional tax, TDS, LWP, other)
  - Net pay
  - Leave balance summary
  - Attendance summary (present, absent, LWP)
- Payslips are retained on the portal for 7 years
- Employees may download PDF copies for their records

## 7. Deductions

### 7.1 Statutory Deductions

| Deduction | Basis | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Provident Fund (PF) | Basic pay | 12% (employee contribution) |
| Professional Tax | Per state slab | As applicable |
| Income Tax (TDS) | As per declared tax regime | As per slab |

### 7.2 Other Deductions

- **Unpaid Leave (LWP)** — daily gross rate × number of LWP days
- **Notice recovery** — for short-notice exits
- **Equipment recovery** — for unreturned or damaged equipment per HR365-POL-004
- **Loan repayment** — where applicable
- **Salary advance recovery** — per agreed schedule

### 7.3 Daily Rate Calculation

`Daily Gross Rate = Monthly Gross Salary / Number of Working Days in Month`

Working days exclude weekends and Company holidays.

## 8. Tax Documents

### 8.1 Form 16

- Issued annually by **June 15** for the preceding financial year
- Available for download on the HR365 Finance portal
- Reflects TDS deducted and salary earned during the financial year

### 8.2 Investment Declarations

- Must be submitted by **January 31** each year via the HR365 Finance portal
- Applies to employees opting for the old tax regime
- Declarations without supporting proof by the deadline may result in higher TDS in the final quarter

### 8.3 Proof Submission

- Investment proofs must be uploaded by **February 15**
- Missing or invalid proofs may result in TDS recovery in the following cycle

## 9. Salary Corrections

### 9.1 Reporting Discrepancies

- Report via the HR365 Finance Helpdesk
- Must be raised within **7 working days** of payslip publication
- Include: the discrepancy, expected correction, and supporting evidence

### 9.2 Resolution Timeline

| Step | SLA |
|---|---|
| Initial acknowledgment | 2 working days |
| Investigation | 5 working days |
| Correction processing | Next payroll cycle |
| Off-cycle payment (urgent) | Finance Head approval required |

### 9.3 Off-Cycle Payments

- Reserved for serious discrepancies (e.g., partial salary not credited)
- Requires Finance Head approval
- Processed within 5 working days of approval
- Tax and PF treatment identical to regular cycle

## 10. Mid-Month Joiners and Exits

- Employees joining or exiting mid-month receive **prorated salary** based on actual working days
- Prorated salary = (Gross Monthly Salary / Working Days in Month) × Working Days Present
- PF, PT, and TDS are prorated accordingly
- Final settlement follows §12

## 11. Salary Advances

- Salary advances are not standard; may be granted on a case-by-case basis
- Requires manager + Finance Head approval
- Maximum: 50% of monthly gross
- Recovery: from the next payroll cycle in a single installment unless agreed otherwise
- No interest is charged on salary advances

## 12. Final Settlement

- Processed within **45 days** of the last working day
- Components:
  - Salary up to last working day
  - Leave encashment (EL up to 30 days, per HR365-POL-003)
  - Statutory dues (PF transfer or withdrawal)
  - Bonus (if applicable per statutory rules)
- Deductions:
  - Notice-period shortfall recovery
  - Unreturned equipment cost
  - Outstanding advances or loans
  - Unpaid leave not yet adjusted

**Statutory compliance:** Full & Final settlement complies with applicable labour laws and the employee's employment agreement.

## 15. Responsibilities

**Employee:** Verify payslips, submit declarations and proofs on time, report discrepancies promptly.
**Manager:** Confirm attendance/leave data accuracy before the 25th cutoff.
**HR:** Provide accurate leave and attendance data to Finance by cutoff.
**Finance:** Process payroll accurately and on schedule; handle corrections and escalations.

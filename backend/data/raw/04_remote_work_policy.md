## Purpose
Defines the rules under which NimbusWorks employees may work from home (WFH) or in a hybrid arrangement.

## Scope
Applies to roles not designated as "on-site only" (e.g., front-desk, lab, or hardware roles are excluded).

## Policy

### 1. Eligibility
- Employees who have completed probation (90 days) are eligible to apply for hybrid or remote work.
- Roles requiring physical presence (as designated by the department head) are not eligible.

### 2. Approval
- WFH requests are submitted via the HR365 portal at least 1 working day in advance for occasional WFH.
- Permanent hybrid/remote arrangements require written manager approval and HR sign-off, reviewed every 6 months.
- Default hybrid allowance (where approved): up to 2 remote days per week.

### 3. Working-Hour Availability
Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours (11:00 AM–4:00 PM) via company-approved chat and video tools, and must maintain the same 9-hour working day as office staff.

### 4. Remote Attendance
Attendance is logged via the HR365 app's WFH Check-In feature (see Attendance Policy, HR365-POL-002, Section 5). Failure to check in/out on a WFH day without prior notice counts as unmarked absence.

### 5. Equipment
- NimbusWorks provides a laptop and, on request, a monitor and headset for confirmed remote employees.
- Equipment remains company property and must be returned within 5 working days of exit or role change to an on-site-only position.

### 6. VPN / Security Requirements
- Company VPN must be used for all access to internal systems (HR365, code repositories, finance tools) while working remotely.
- Personal devices may not be used to access company email or internal systems; see IT Security Policy (HR365-POL-008) for full requirements.
- Public Wi-Fi use for company work requires an active VPN connection.

## Exceptions
Temporary WFH due to medical reasons or local emergencies may be approved by the manager without the standard 1-day advance notice, subject to HR notification within 24 hours.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: maintain availability during core hours and follow VPN/security rules.
- Managers: review recurring remote arrangements every 6 months.
- IT Department: provision and reclaim equipment and VPN access.

## Contact / Escalation
Remote work queries: hr@nimbusworks.example. IT/VPN issues: it-support@nimbusworks.example.

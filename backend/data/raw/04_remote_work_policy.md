## 1. Purpose

Defines the rules under which NimbusWorks employees may work from home (WFH) or in a hybrid arrangement, including eligibility, approval, attendance, security, and equipment obligations.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all roles not designated "on-site only." On-site-only roles (front-desk, lab, hardware, facilities) are excluded by department head designation. Applies to all office locations and remote arrangements.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| WFH | Work From Home — ad-hoc or scheduled remote day |
| Hybrid | Recurring arrangement of office + remote days |
| Fully Remote | Role where 100% of work is performed remotely |
| On-Site Only | Role that requires physical presence per department head designation |
| Core Hours | 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM — mandatory reachability window |
| WFH Check-In | The HR365 app feature that logs WFH start and end |
| VPN | Company Virtual Private Network — mandatory for internal systems |
| Advance Notice | Working days before the WFH day when the request must be submitted |

## 4. Policy Statement

NimbusWorks supports flexible work where the role allows, subject to business needs, team coverage, and security requirements. Remote work is a privilege, not an entitlement, and may be modified or withdrawn based on performance, business need, or policy violation.

## 5. Eligibility

| Employee Status | Eligible? |
|---|---|
| Confirmed employee in an eligible role | Yes |
| Probationary employee | No (except ad-hoc WFH with manager approval in the last 30 days of probation) |
| Contract employee (>90 days) | Per engagement agreement |
| Intern | Per HR365-POL-012 |

Roles are classified as eligible or on-site only by the department head in consultation with HR. Classification is visible on the HR365 portal.

## 6. Approval

| Type | Advance Notice | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Ad-hoc WFH (single day) | 1 working day | Direct manager |
| Recurring hybrid (weekly pattern) | 5 working days | Direct manager + HR sign-off |
| Fully remote | 15 working days | Department head + HR Head |
| Emergency WFH (medical/emergency) | Same day | Manager (HR notified within 24 hours) |

Approvals are reviewed:

- Hybrid: every 6 months
- Fully remote: every 6 months
- Ad-hoc: per instance

## 7. Working-Hour Availability

- Remote employees must maintain the **same 9-hour working day** as office staff
- Must be reachable during **core hours (11:00 AM – 4:00 PM)**
- Must respond to chat within 30 minutes and email within 4 hours during working hours
- Must attend scheduled meetings with video where the meeting host requires it

## 8. Remote Attendance

- Attendance is logged via the HR365 app's **WFH Check-In** feature
- Both **start-of-day** and **end-of-day** confirmation are mandatory
- Failure to check in/out without prior notice counts as **unmarked absence** (see HR365-POL-002)
- WFH presence rules match office rules: 8 hours minimum for Present

## 9. Equipment

| Item | Provided? | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop | Yes | All employees |
| Monitor | On request | Confirmed remote/hybrid employees |
| Headset | On request | Confirmed remote/hybrid employees |
| Keyboard / mouse | On request | Confirmed remote/hybrid employees |

- Equipment remains Company property
- Must be returned within **5 working days** of exit or role change to on-site only
- Damage due to negligence may be recovered per HR365-POL-007

## 10. VPN and Security

- **Company VPN is mandatory** for all access to internal systems (HR365, code repos, finance tools) while remote
- **Personal devices** may not be used for company email or internal systems (see HR365-POL-008)
- **Public Wi-Fi** use requires an active VPN
- MFA is mandatory for all remote access
- Suspicious activity must be reported to it-security@nimbusworks.example

## 11. Workspace Standards

Employees are expected to:

- Work from a quiet, private location
- Maintain a stable internet connection (minimum 20 Mbps)
- Have a functional camera and mic for meetings
- Ensure the workspace allows full engagement in meetings and calls

Employees working from non-standard locations (cafés, co-working) must still use VPN and follow device rules.

## 12. WFH Location

Employees are expected to WFH from their declared residence. WFH from another city, state, or country requires prior HR approval for:

- Tax and compliance reasons
- Data protection obligations
- Insurance coverage

Unapproved remote work from another jurisdiction may result in disciplinary action.

## 13. Exceptions

Temporary WFH due to medical reasons or local emergencies may be approved by the manager without the standard 1-day advance notice, subject to HR notification within 24 hours.

Extended medical WFH (beyond 15 days) requires HR approval and may require medical documentation per HR365-POL-003.

## 15. Responsibilities

**Employee:** Maintain availability, follow VPN/security rules, log attendance accurately.
**Manager:** Review recurring arrangements every 6 months, ensure team coverage, action ad-hoc requests within SLA.
**HR:** Approve recurring/fully remote arrangements, notify on policy changes, maintain classification records.
**IT:** Provision and reclaim equipment; maintain VPN and endpoint protection.

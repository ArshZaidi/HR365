# Company Notices

- Document ID: HR365-POL-011
- Document Type: Notice Collection
- Authority: Time-bounded — individual notice expiry controls retrieval
- Version: 1.0
- Effective Date: 2026-01-01
- Review Date: 2026-12-31
- Owner: HR Department
- Approved By: Head of HR
- Status: Active
- Applies To: All employees (unless a specific notice states otherwise)
- Confidentiality: Internal
- Supersedes: None

## Purpose
Hosts time-sensitive announcements and circulars for NimbusWorks employees. Unlike the other policy documents, this file is expected to change frequently. Each notice carries its own metadata and expiry date. The ingestion pipeline must exclude notices whose expiry date has passed from the active retrieval corpus while retaining them for historical/audit reference.

## Scope
Applies to all employees for company-wide notices; department-specific notices apply only to the stated audience.

## Policy

### Notice Format
Every notice in this file must include: Notice ID, Title, Published Date, Expiry Date, Priority, Audience, Content, and Contact.

## Exceptions
Notices marked "High" priority may be pinned to the top of the HR365 portal notice board regardless of publish date, until their expiry date.

## Responsibilities
- HR Department: publish notices with complete metadata and ensure expired notices are excluded from the active retrieval corpus while retained for historical/audit reference.
- Employees: review notices relevant to their department/audience.

## 1. Purpose

Hosts time-sensitive announcements and circulars for NimbusWorks employees. Unlike the other policy documents, this file is **expected to change frequently**. Each notice carries its own metadata and expiry date.

## 2. Ingestion Rules

- **Active corpus:** Include notices whose Expiry Date is on or after the current date.
- **Historical corpus:** Retain expired notices for audit and historical reference — but **exclude** them from active retrieval.
- **Priority pinning:** Notices marked **High** priority may be pinned to the top of the portal notice board until their expiry date.
- **Notice ID format:** `HR365-NTC-YYYY-NNN` (year + 3-digit sequence)

## 3. Notice Format

Every notice in this file must include:

- Notice ID
- Title
- Published Date
- Expiry Date
- Retrieval Status (Active / Expired — exclude from active retrieval)
- Priority (Low / Medium / High)
- Audience
- Content
- Contact

## 5. Exceptions

Notices marked "High" priority may be pinned to the top of the HR365 portal notice board regardless of publish date, until their expiry date.

## 6. Responsibilities

- **HR Department:** Publish notices with complete metadata; ensure expired notices are excluded from the active retrieval corpus while retained for historical/audit reference.
- **Employees:** Review notices relevant to their department/audience.

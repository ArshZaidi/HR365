## 1. Purpose

Defines how workplace safety concerns and grievances (including harassment complaints) are reported, investigated, and resolved at NimbusWorks.

## 2. Scope

- **Safety:** All employees, contractors, and visitors to NimbusWorks premises
- **Grievance:** All employees (including contractors where applicable)

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Safety Hazard | Any condition that risks injury, illness, or property damage |
| Grievance | A formal complaint about workplace conduct, decisions, or conditions |
| Harassment | Unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic, or that creates a hostile environment |
| Internal Committee (IC) | Committee constituted per applicable law for harassment matters |
| Fact-Finding Review | Structured investigation into a grievance |
| Need-to-Know | Principle of limiting information access to those who require it |
| Anonymous Report | A complaint submitted without identifying the reporter |
| Retaliation | Adverse action against a reporter or witness |
| Escalation | Referral of an unresolved matter to a higher authority |

## 4. Safety Reporting

### 4.1 What to Report

Any workplace safety hazard, including:

- Fire risks (blocked exits, faulty wiring)
- Electrical hazards
- Structural issues
- Ergonomic risks (poor seating, inadequate lighting)
- Chemical or biological exposures
- Slip, trip, and fall hazards
- Violence or threat of violence
- Any unsafe condition observed on premises

### 4.2 How to Report

- **HR365 Safety Report form** (primary channel)
- **Directly to the on-site Safety Officer**
- Urgent safety risks must be reported **immediately**

### 4.3 Safety Officer Responsibilities

- Conduct a facility walkthrough **monthly**
- Log findings in the Safety Register
- Ensure corrective actions are tracked to closure
- Coordinate emergency drills
- Maintain emergency contact lists

### 4.4 Safety Register

- Maintained by the Safety Officer
- Contains all reported hazards, actions taken, and status
- Reviewed monthly by HR and department heads
- Retained for 7 years

### 4.5 Emergency Procedures

| Emergency | Action |
|---|---|
| Fire | Evacuate via designated exits; do not use elevators |
| Medical | Contact on-site First Responder; call emergency services |
| Security | Report to security and IT Security |
| Natural disaster | Follow instructions from Safety Officers and local authorities |

Emergency drills are conducted at least twice per year.

## 5. Harassment Reporting

### 5.1 What Constitutes Harassment

- Unwelcome physical contact
- Sexual advances or requests for favors
- Verbal conduct (slurs, jokes, threats) based on protected characteristics
- Non-verbal conduct (displaying offensive materials, gestures)
- Quid pro quo conduct (implicit or explicit demands in return for benefits)
- Any conduct creating an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment

### 5.2 Reporting Channels

- **HR365 Grievance module**
- **Directly with HR**
- **Internal Committee (IC)** — for harassment matters per applicable law
- **Anonymous Ethics Hotline** — where anonymity is preferred

### 5.3 Acknowledgment

- Complaints are acknowledged within **2 working days**
- Reporter is assigned a case reference number
- Reporter is informed of next steps and timelines

### 5.4 Confidentiality

All grievance and harassment matters are handled on a **strict need-to-know basis**. Details are not shared with colleagues outside the investigation team. Identities of the reporter, respondent, and witnesses are protected to the extent permitted by law.

## 6. Grievance Process

### 6.1 Step-by-Step

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee raises grievance via HR365 portal or directly to HR/IC | — |
| 2 | HR/IC conducts fact-finding review | Within 10 working days |
| 3 | Findings and recommended action communicated in writing | On completion of review |
| 4 | Employee may request review by HR Head if dissatisfied | Within 5 working days of decision |

### 6.2 Fact-Finding Review

- Conducted by HR or IC (for harassment matters)
- Interview-based with all relevant parties
- Documentation collected (emails, screenshots, witness statements)
- Findings documented in writing
- Independence of the investigation is maintained

### 6.3 Outcome Options

- No further action (complaint not substantiated)
- Mediation or facilitated conversation
- Coaching or counseling
- Formal warning (verbal or written)
- Suspension
- Termination
- Referral to legal authorities (for criminal conduct)

### 6.4 Communication of Outcome

- Provided in writing to the reporter
- Reasons are shared to the extent confidentiality permits
- Reporter is informed of escalation rights

## 7. Urgent and Sensitive Escalation

Matters involving **immediate safety risk** or **serious misconduct** are escalated to:

- HR Head
- Internal Committee (where harassment-related)
- Executive leadership (where enterprise-level risk exists)
- Law enforcement (where legally required)

Escalation occurs within **24 hours** of the report.

## 8. Non-Retaliation

NimbusWorks prohibits retaliation against any employee who:

- Reports a concern in good faith
- Participates in an investigation
- Supports a colleague in raising a concern

Retaliation is itself grounds for disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## 9. Anonymous Reports

- Anonymous reports are accepted via the Ethics Hotline
- Investigation proceeds to the extent possible without identifying the reporter
- **Limitation:** Anonymous reports may restrict follow-up if the reporter cannot be contacted for clarification
- Anonymous reports are logged and tracked to closure

## 10. Record Keeping

- All grievances and outcomes are logged in the HR365 case management system
- Records retained for 7 years
- Access restricted to HR and, where applicable, IC members
- Statistical summaries (without identifying details) are shared with leadership for review

## 11. Timeline Summary

| Stage | SLA |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgment | 2 working days |
| Fact-finding review | 10 working days |
| Communication of findings | On completion |
| Review by HR Head | 5 working days from request |
| Urgent escalation | Within 24 hours |

## 12. Responsibilities

**Employee:** Report safety hazards and grievances promptly and in good faith.
**Safety Officers:** Conduct monthly inspections; maintain the Safety Register.
**Manager:** Escalate safety concerns; do not attempt independent investigation of grievances.
**HR / Internal Committee:** Investigate impartially within stated timelines; maintain confidentiality.
**HR Head:** Act as escalation authority for unresolved or sensitive matters.

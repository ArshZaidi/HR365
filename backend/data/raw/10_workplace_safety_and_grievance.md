## Purpose
Defines how workplace safety concerns and grievances (including harassment complaints) are reported, investigated, and resolved at NimbusWorks.

## Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors to NimbusWorks premises for safety matters; applies to all employees for grievance matters.

## Policy

### 1. Safety Reporting
- Any workplace safety hazard (fire, electrical, structural, ergonomic) must be reported immediately via the HR365 Safety Report form or to the on-site Safety Officer.
- Safety Officers conduct a facility walkthrough monthly and log findings in the Safety Register.

### 2. Harassment Reporting
- Harassment complaints (including sexual harassment) may be filed via the HR365 Grievance module, directly with HR, or with the Internal Committee (IC) constituted per applicable law.
- Complaints are acknowledged within 2 working days.

### 3. Grievance Process
- Step 1: Employee raises the grievance via the HR365 portal or directly to HR/IC.
- Step 2: HR/IC conducts a fact-finding review, typically within 10 working days.
- Step 3: Findings and recommended action are communicated to the employee in writing.
- Step 4: Employee may request a review by the HR Head if dissatisfied with the outcome, within 5 working days of the decision.

### 4. Confidentiality
All grievance and harassment matters are handled on a strict need-to-know basis. Details are not shared with colleagues outside the investigation team.

### 5. Urgent / Sensitive Escalation
Matters involving immediate safety risk or serious misconduct are escalated to the HR Head and, where required, to the Internal Committee within 24 hours of the report.

## Exceptions
Anonymous reports via the Ethics Hotline are accepted but may limit the extent of follow-up possible if the reporter cannot be contacted for clarification.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: report safety hazards and grievances promptly and in good faith.
- Safety Officers: conduct regular inspections and maintain the Safety Register.
- HR / Internal Committee: investigate grievances impartially within stated timelines.

## Contact / Escalation
Safety: safety@nimbusworks.example. Grievance/Harassment: grievance@nimbusworks.example or the HR365 Grievance module. Urgent escalation: HR Head, hrhead@nimbusworks.example.

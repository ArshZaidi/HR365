## Purpose
Defines the authoritative rules for marking, tracking, and correcting employee attendance at NimbusWorks.

## Scope
Applies to all employees, including those on hybrid or remote work arrangements.

## Policy

### 1. Present / Absent / Half-Day Definitions
- **Present**: Logged in for at least 8 hours (including breaks) between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM.
- **Half-Day**: Logged in for between 4 and 6 hours in a single day.
- **Absent**: Logged in for less than 4 hours with no approved leave on record.

### 2. Late Arrival and Late-Mark Rules
- Check-in after 9:45 AM is marked "Late."
- 3 late marks in a calendar month convert to a 0.5-day leave deduction.
- 2 or more late marks beyond that in the same month require manager sign-off; repeated lateness may be escalated per the Code of Conduct (HR365-POL-005).

### 3. Check-In / Check-Out
- All employees must check in and check out daily via the HR365 mobile app or office biometric device.
- Missed check-outs default to a system-assigned check-out time of 6:30 PM, flagged for review.

### 4. Shift Rules
- Standard shift: 9:30 AM–6:30 PM.
- Support/Operations staff on rotational shifts follow a published roster; shift start times may vary between 7:00 AM and 10:00 PM.
- A minimum 11-hour gap is required between the end of one shift and the start of the next.

### 5. WFH Attendance
- Remote work days are logged via the HR365 app's "WFH Check-In" feature, which requires a start-of-day and end-of-day confirmation.
- WFH attendance is subject to the same 8-hour presence rule as office attendance.

### 6. Attendance Corrections
- Correction requests must be submitted within 5 working days of the discrepancy via the HR365 portal, with a reason.
- Manager approval is required for all corrections; HR audits corrections monthly.

### 7. Exceptions and Approvals
- Field visits, client meetings, and official travel are logged as "On Duty" with prior manager approval and do not count as absence.
- Medical emergencies preventing check-in must be reported to the manager within 24 hours and regularized within 5 working days.

## Exceptions
Employees on approved leave (see Leave Policy, HR365-POL-003) are not marked absent for the leave period.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: check in/out daily and raise correction requests promptly.
- Managers: approve or reject corrections within 3 working days.
- HR Department: conduct monthly attendance audits.

## Contact / Escalation
Attendance queries: attendance-support@nimbusworks.example. Unresolved corrections may be escalated to the HR Helpdesk (extension 4001).

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the authoritative rules for marking, tracking, correcting, and auditing employee attendance at NimbusWorks Pvt. Ltd. ("the Company"). It establishes:

- The definitions of Present, Half-Day, Absent, Late, and On Duty
- The check-in / check-out mechanics for office, remote, and shift-based work
- The late-mark system and its consequences
- The shift roster rules and minimum rest requirements
- The attendance correction workflow and audit rules
- The exceptions framework for field visits, medical emergencies, and travel
- The reporting obligations of managers, HR, and employees
- The escalation paths for unresolved attendance matters

This document is the authoritative source for all attendance matters. Where the HR FAQ (HR365-POL-009) or any summary document differs, this policy overrides.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All full-time employees
- All part-time employees (with prorated presence thresholds — see §6.3)
- All contract employees on engagements exceeding 90 days
- All office locations of NimbusWorks
- All remote and hybrid work arrangements
- All rotational shift staff (subject to §7)

This policy does **not** apply to:

- Independent consultants engaged via statement-of-work
- Interns (governed by HR365-POL-012)
- Visitors, vendors, or clients on premises

## 4. Policy Statement

Accurate attendance records are essential for:

- Payroll processing
- Leave balance calculations
- Compliance with statutory requirements
- Team capacity planning
- Performance and productivity assessment
- Business continuity during critical periods

Every employee is responsible for marking their own attendance daily and for raising any correction requests within the stated window. Managers are responsible for reviewing their team's attendance and approving corrections within SLA. HR is responsible for auditing records monthly and reporting exceptions.

No employee may mark attendance on behalf of another employee. Attendance fraud is a Code of Conduct (HR365-POL-005) violation and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 6. Present, Half-Day, Absent, and Short-Day Definitions

### 6.1 Present

An employee is marked Present if the total logged time for the day is **at least 8 hours** within the 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM window.

- Breaks (lunch and short breaks) are counted within the 8 hours
- Time before 8:00 AM and after 8:00 PM does not count toward the 8-hour threshold unless pre-approved by the manager
- Multiple sessions within the day can be combined (e.g., check-in 9:00 AM, check-out 12:00 PM, check-in 1:00 PM, check-out 6:30 PM = 8.5 hours total)

### 6.2 Half-Day

An employee is marked Half-Day if the total logged time for the day is **between 4 and 6 hours**.

- 0.5 day is deducted from the leave balance (CL) or treated as LWP if no CL balance
- Half-day requests must be pre-approved by the manager
- Late arrivals that reduce logged time to under 6 hours may trigger an automatic half-day designation

### 6.3 Short Day

An employee is marked Short Day if the total logged time for the day is **between 6 and 8 hours**.

- 0.25 day is deducted from the leave balance (CL) or treated as LWP if no CL balance
- Short Day is not an automatic manager designation — it's calculated by the system
- Repeated Short Days may trigger manager discussion about workload or schedule

### 6.4 Absent

An employee is marked Absent if:

- Total logged time for the day is **less than 4 hours**, AND
- No approved leave (SL, CL, EL, BL, etc.) covers the day, AND
- No approved On Duty record exists

Absent days are:

- Deducted from payroll as LWP at the daily gross rate
- Counted in the monthly absence report shared with managers
- Escalated to HR if 2 or more unmarked absences occur in a single month

### 6.5 Week-Off and Holiday

| Day Type | Attendance Required? | Payroll Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Week-Off | No | N/A (standard) |
| Holiday (declared) | No | N/A (paid) |
| Week-Off worked (pre-approved) | Yes — Comp-Off earned | Comp-Off credited |
| Holiday worked (pre-approved) | Yes — Comp-Off earned | Comp-Off credited |

## 7. Standard Working Hours

### 7.1 Core Schedule

| Element | Timing |
|---|---|
| Standard Shift Start | 9:30 AM |
| Grace Period Ends | 9:45 AM |
| Lunch Break | 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM (or flexible 1-hour within 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM with manager approval) |
| Core Collaboration Hours | 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM |
| Standard Shift End | 6:30 PM |
| Late Check-Out Threshold | After 6:30 PM (no penalty, no credit for regular work) |

### 7.2 Working Days

Standard working days are **Monday to Friday**. Saturday and Sunday are week-offs unless:

- The employee is on a rotational shift roster that includes weekend work
- A special event requires weekend presence, in which case the manager publishes the schedule at least 7 days in advance

### 7.3 Breaks

- **Lunch:** 1 hour (flexible within 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM)
- **Morning break:** 15 minutes (typically around 11:00 AM)
- **Afternoon break:** 15 minutes (typically around 4:00 PM)
- Breaks may not be combined to leave early
- Breaks must be taken within the standard shift hours

### 7.4 Core Hours

Between **11:00 AM and 4:00 PM**, all employees must be:

- Reachable via company chat (Slack, Teams, or equivalent)
- Available for calls and meetings
- Responsive to messages within 30 minutes

Employees unable to be reachable during core hours should:

- Block the time in their calendar
- Notify the manager in advance (for planned unavailability)
- For unplanned unavailability, notify the manager as soon as possible

## 8. Late Arrival and Late-Mark Rules

### 8.1 Late Definition

An employee is marked Late if:

- Check-in is **after 9:45 AM** for the standard shift
- Check-in is **after the shift-start + 15 minutes** for shift-based staff

### 8.2 Late-Mark Consequences

| Late Marks in a Month | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Warning only; no deduction |
| 3 | 0.5-day CL deduction |
| 4–5 | 1-day CL deduction + manager discussion |
| 6+ | 1-day CL deduction + written warning + escalation to HR |

**Additional rules:**

- Two or more late marks beyond the 3-mark threshold in the same month require manager sign-off
- Repeated lateness in consecutive months is escalated per the Code of Conduct (HR365-POL-005)
- Late marks do not reset mid-month; they accumulate for the calendar month

### 8.3 Exceptions to Late Marking

Late arrival is **not** marked if:

- Pre-approved by the manager (e.g., a scheduled medical appointment)
- Due to a documented transport disruption or emergency (must be reported within 2 hours)
- The employee was on approved On Duty or travel on that day
- The employee was on WFH and the manager accepted the actual start time

### 8.4 Late-Mark Reporting

- Late marks are visible on the ESS portal in real time
- Monthly late-mark summaries are shared with managers on the 1st of the following month
- Employees may dispute late marks via the correction process (§11)

## 9. Check-In / Check-Out

### 9.1 Methods

Attendance can be marked via:

- **HR365 mobile app** — for both office and remote work
- **Office biometric device** — for on-site attendance
- **Web ESS portal** — for WFH check-in

### 9.2 Check-In Requirements

- Check-in must occur within the standard shift start window (9:30 AM – 9:45 AM) to avoid Late marking
- Employees unable to check in at the usual time must notify their manager per §9.5
- Check-in via colleague's device is prohibited and treated as fraud

### 9.3 Check-Out Requirements

- Check-out must occur at the end of the shift or when leaving the premises
- **Missed check-out:** The system assigns a default check-out time of 6:30 PM
- Missed check-outs are flagged for review and may be corrected via §11
- Repeated missed check-outs (3 or more in a month) trigger a manager conversation

### 9.4 Multiple Sessions

Multiple check-in / check-out pairs in a day are permitted (e.g., for split shifts, off-site meetings). Total logged time is summed across all sessions for the day.

### 9.5 Failed Check-In

If the biometric or mobile app fails to register attendance:

1. Notify the manager within 2 hours
2. Raise a correction request via §11 within 5 working days
3. Provide supporting evidence (e.g., a screenshot, network logs, or manager confirmation)

## 10. Shift Rules

### 10.1 Standard Shift

The standard shift is **9:30 AM – 6:30 PM**, Monday to Friday.

### 10.2 Rotational Shifts

Rotational shifts apply to Support, Operations, and any team designated by a department head.

- **Shift windows:** Between 7:00 AM and 10:00 PM
- **Shift roster:** Published at least **14 days in advance** on the HR365 portal
- **Change requests:** Employees may request shift swaps at least **5 working days** in advance, with manager approval

### 10.3 Shift Rest Requirements

- **Minimum gap between shifts:** 11 hours
- **Maximum continuous shift:** 10 hours
- **Maximum consecutive shifts:** 6 days without a week-off

Any shift assignment that would violate these rules requires HR approval.

### 10.4 Shift Compensation

- Night shifts (10:00 PM – 6:00 AM): Applicable shift allowance per Payroll Policy (HR365-POL-007)
- Weekend shifts: Comp-Off earned per Leave Policy (HR365-POL-003, §7.7)
- Holiday shifts: Comp-Off earned, plus holiday pay as applicable

## 11. Attendance Corrections

### 11.1 When to Raise a Correction Request

Corrections are needed when:

- A check-in or check-out was missed
- The recorded time is incorrect
- Attendance is marked Absent but the employee was on approved leave or On Duty
- A day is unmarked due to system error

### 11.2 Correction Window

- Corrections must be raised within **5 working days** of the discrepancy
- Requests beyond 5 working days require HR approval
- Requests beyond 30 days are not accepted except in exceptional circumstances

### 11.3 Correction Request Process

1. Navigate to ESS → "My Attendance" → "Raise Correction"
2. Select the date and reason
3. Upload supporting evidence (e.g., manager note, screenshot, appointment slip)
4. Submit — the manager receives a notification

### 11.4 Manager SLA

Managers must action correction requests within **3 working days**. Auto-escalation applies after that.

### 11.5 Manager Rejection

Managers may reject a correction request only with a documented reason. Rejections are visible to HR for audit.

### 11.6 HR Audit

HR audits attendance corrections monthly. Patterns of abuse (e.g., repeated corrections for the same issue, corrections without supporting evidence) are escalated.

## 12. WFH Attendance

### 12.1 WFH Check-In

Remote work is logged via the HR365 app's "WFH Check-In" feature:

- **Start-of-day:** Check-in within the first 30 minutes of shift start
- **End-of-day:** Check-out at shift end or when logging off
- Both are mandatory for a WFH day to count as Present

### 12.2 WFH Presence Rules

WFH attendance is subject to the **same 8-hour presence rule** as office attendance. WFH employees must:

- Be reachable during core hours (11:00 AM – 4:00 PM)
- Log time via the app in real time
- Respond to manager messages within 30 minutes during core hours

### 12.3 Failure to WFH Check-In

If an employee fails to log WFH check-in / check-out without prior notice:

- The day counts as **unmarked absence**
- Payroll deduction applies unless corrected per §11

### 12.4 WFH Location

Employees are expected to WFH from their declared residence. WFH from another city or country requires prior HR approval for tax and compliance reasons.

## 13. Exceptions

### 13.1 Field Visits and Client Meetings

Field visits and client meetings are logged as **On Duty (OD)** with prior manager approval. On Duty days:

- Do not count as absence
- Do not require physical check-in
- Must be reported in advance via the ESS portal
- Must be substantiated with visit notes if requested

### 13.2 Official Travel

Travel on company business is:

- Logged as OD
- Subject to travel policy for reimbursements (per HR365-POL-006)
- Not counted for late-marking purposes

### 13.3 Medical Emergencies

If a medical emergency prevents check-in:

- Notify the manager within **24 hours**
- Regularize the attendance via §11 within **5 working days**
- Attach medical documentation if applicable

### 13.4 Transport Disruptions

Documented transport disruptions (e.g., metro strike, flight cancellation) may be accepted as a valid reason for late arrival, provided the employee notifies the manager within 2 hours.

### 13.5 System Downtime

If the HR365 app or biometric system is down:

- Notify the manager and IT
- The manager manually records attendance for affected employees
- Corrections are auto-approved once system functionality is restored

## 18. Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: What counts as a full day?**
A: 8 hours of logged time within 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM, including breaks.

**Q: What happens if I forget to check out?**
A: The system assigns 6:30 PM as your default check-out. You can correct via §11 if needed.

**Q: Can I check in from home?**
A: Only if you are approved for WFH and using the WFH Check-In feature.

**Q: Can I mark attendance for a colleague?**
A: No. This is fraud and a Code of Conduct violation.

**Q: How many hours do I need to work daily?**
A: 8 hours minimum for Present. Short Days (6–8 hours) deduct 0.25 CL. Half-Days (4–6 hours) deduct 0.5 CL.

### Late Arrival

**Q: What time is considered late?**
A: Check-in after 9:45 AM is marked Late.

**Q: How many late marks can I have before consequences?**
A: 1–2 is warning-only. 3 converts to a 0.5-day CL deduction. 4–5 results in 1-day CL deduction + manager discussion. 6+ results in 1-day CL deduction + written warning.

**Q: Can I get late marks waived?**
A: Yes, with manager pre-approval for a specific reason, or if the delay is due to a documented disruption (transport strike, medical emergency).

**Q: What if I'm late but stay later?**
A: Staying later doesn't automatically remove the late mark. But it counts toward your total logged hours.

**Q: Do late marks reset monthly?**
A: Yes. Late marks accumulate within a calendar month and reset on the 1st.

### Presence and Half-Days

**Q: What's the minimum I can work and still count as present?**
A: 4 hours for a half-day. Below 4 hours counts as Absent.

**Q: How do I apply for a half-day?**
A: Use the ESS portal → "Apply for Leave" → CL → half-day checkbox. Manager pre-approval required.

**Q: What if I work 6.5 hours?**
A: That counts as a Short Day → 0.25 CL deduction.

**Q: Can I combine morning and evening sessions?**
A: Yes. Total logged time for the day is summed across all sessions.

### WFH

**Q: Do I need to check in when working from home?**
A: Yes. The WFH Check-In feature is required for both start-of-day and end-of-day.

**Q: What if I forget WFH check-in?**
A: The day counts as unmarked absence. Raise a correction request with manager approval within 5 working days.

**Q: Can I WFH for a half-day?**
A: Yes, with manager approval. Both start and end check-ins are still required.

**Q: Can I WFH from a different city?**
A: Not without prior HR approval for tax and compliance reasons.

### Shifts

**Q: How do I know my shift?**
A: Check the ESS portal → "My Shift." Rosters are published 14 days in advance.

**Q: Can I swap shifts?**
A: Yes, with manager approval at least 5 working days in advance.

**Q: What's the minimum rest between shifts?**
A: 11 hours.

**Q: How many consecutive shifts can I work?**
A: 6 days without a week-off.

### Corrections

**Q: How long do I have to correct attendance?**
A: 5 working days from the discrepancy.

**Q: What evidence do I need?**
A: A reason and, for complex cases, supporting evidence (manager note, screenshot, appointment slip).

**Q: What if my manager rejects my correction?**
A: Escalate to HR Helpdesk. If unresolved, it goes to the HR Ops Manager.

**Q: Can I correct attendance after 30 days?**
A: Only with HR approval and exceptional circumstances.

### On Duty and Travel

**Q: How do I mark On Duty?**
A: Apply via the ESS portal → "Apply for On Duty" with manager approval.

**Q: Does travel count as Present?**
A: Yes. Travel logged as On Duty counts as a full day.

**Q: What if I travel overnight?**
A: Log the travel days as On Duty and confirm arrival/departure times in the OD record.

## 19. Worked Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Standard Day

**Employee:** On-site, standard shift.

**Event:** Checks in at 9:35 AM, checks out at 6:35 PM (with a 1-hour lunch).

**Result:** 8 hours logged = Present. No late mark (check-in before 9:45 AM).

### Scenario 2: Traffic Delay

**Employee:** Standard shift, commuter.

**Event:** Checks in at 10:05 AM due to traffic; notifies the manager at 9:50 AM with a screenshot of the delay.

**Result:** Late mark applied. Manager may waive the late mark if the reason is documented and consistent with §8.3.

### Scenario 3: Medical Appointment

**Employee:** Confirmed, standard shift.

**Event:** Pre-approved 3-hour medical appointment in the morning; arrives at 12:30 PM.

**Result:**
- Morning portion (9:30 AM – 12:30 PM) is Half-Day (0.5 CL deduction, pre-approved)
- Afternoon portion (12:30 PM – 6:30 PM) is Present
- Total: Half-Day + Present = combined 0.5-day deduction

### Scenario 4: WFH with Failed Check-In

**Employee:** Approved for WFH.

**Event:** WFH check-in fails at 9:30 AM due to app glitch; reports it to manager at 9:45 AM; IT restores at 10:30 AM; check-in registered at 10:35 AM.

**Result:**
- Total logged time: 10:35 AM – 6:30 PM = 7.9 hours → Short Day
- Correction request via §11 to add 9:30 AM check-in with IT confirmation
- If corrected: full-day Present

### Scenario 5: Late Arrival with Late Stay

**Employee:** Standard shift.

**Event:** Checks in at 10:15 AM (45 minutes late) but checks out at 7:15 PM (45 minutes later than standard).

**Result:** Late mark applied. Late stay does not remove the mark. The employee may submit a correction request for a manager-approved flex start.

### Scenario 6: Rotational Shift with Rest Violation

**Employee:** Support staff on rotational shifts.

**Event:** Assigned to a shift ending at 11:00 PM and next shift starting at 9:00 AM the following day (10-hour gap).

**Result:** Roster violation — minimum 11-hour gap not met. Manager must re-issue the roster. HR notified.

### Scenario 7: Missed Check-Out

**Employee:** Standard shift, on-site.

**Event:** Forgets to check out at 6:30 PM.

**Result:** System assigns 6:30 PM as default. Employee can raise a correction if the actual check-out was different.

### Scenario 8: Repeated Late Arrivals

**Employee:** Confirmed, standard shift.

**Event:** Late on the 4th, 9th, 15th, and 22nd of the same month.

**Result:** 4 late marks → 1-day CL deduction + manager discussion. HR notified.

### Scenario 9: Absence Without Leave

**Employee:** Confirmed, standard shift.

**Event:** No check-in on a Tuesday; no leave applied; no notification.

**Result:**
- Day marked Absent
- 1 day LWP deducted from payroll
- Manager notifies HR if 2 or more unmarked absences in the month

### Scenario 10: Public Holiday WFH

**Employee:** Confirmed, on WFH.

**Event:** Works from home on a declared public holiday.

**Result:** No attendance required (holiday). If pre-approved for Comp-Off, Comp-Off credited per Leave Policy §7.7.

### Scenario 11: Field Visit

**Employee:** Sales.

**Event:** Client visit scheduled 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM. Logs OD via the portal.

**Result:** On Duty for the day. No check-in required. Full-day credit.

### Scenario 12: Shift Swap

**Employee:** Rotational shift.

**Event:** Wants to swap Saturday's shift with a colleague's Sunday shift.

**Result:**
- Submit shift-swap request at least 5 working days in advance
- Both employees' managers confirm the swap
- Roster updated on the ESS portal

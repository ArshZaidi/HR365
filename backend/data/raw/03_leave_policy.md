## 1. Purpose

This policy defines NimbusWorks Pvt. Ltd. ("the Company") rules for employee leave. It establishes:

- The categories of leave available to employees and their entitlements
- The eligibility rules for each leave type, including probationary status
- The accrual, crediting, carry-forward, and lapse mechanics
- The application, approval, and cancellation workflows via the HR365 portal
- The documentation and medical-certificate requirements
- The interaction between leave and other policies (attendance, payroll, remote work)
- The escalation paths and SLAs for unresolved leave matters
- The reporting and audit obligations of the HR Department

This document is the authoritative source for all leave matters. Where another document (including the HR FAQ, HR365-POL-009) summarizes leave rules, this policy overrides in any conflict.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All full-time employees, confirmed and probationary
- All part-time employees, with prorated entitlements per §6.5
- All contract employees engaged on rolling assignments exceeding 90 calendar days
- All office locations of NimbusWorks
- All remote and hybrid work arrangements

This policy does **not** apply to:

- Independent consultants engaged via statement-of-work contracts (their leave is governed by their engagement agreement)
- Interns (governed by the Internship Policy, HR365-POL-012)
- Contract staff on assignments shorter than 90 days

## 4. Policy Statement

NimbusWorks provides paid leave to support employee wellbeing, personal obligations, and health needs. Leave is an entitlement, not a discretionary benefit — but it is also a planned absence that must be coordinated to maintain business continuity.

Every leave request is subject to:

1. Availability of sufficient balance (or advance eligibility per §6.6)
2. Notification and documentation requirements (§11)
3. Manager approval (§10)
4. Consistent application across all employees regardless of role or seniority

No employee may be penalized for taking leave they are entitled to, and no manager may unreasonably withhold approval. Where business needs conflict with requested leave, the escalation path in §17 applies.

## 6. Eligibility, Accrual, and Balance Mechanics

### 6.1 Eligibility by Employment Status

| Employee Status | CL | SL | EL | ML | PL | BL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Probationary | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| Confirmed | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Part-time (<30 hrs/week) | Prorated | Prorated | Prorated | Prorated | Prorated | Full |
| Contract (>90 days) | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ | Per agreement | Per agreement | ✓ |

### 6.2 Annual Allowance

The allowance for each leave type is as stated in §5. Allowances are **per leave year** (Jan 1 – Dec 31). Employees joining mid-year receive prorated allowances per §6.5.

### 6.3 Accrual Rules

**Earned Leave (EL)** is the only leave type that accrues. It:

- Accrues at **1.25 days per calendar month**
- Is credited on the **1st of each month** to the employee's available balance
- Accrues only during months in which the employee has at least **15 days** of present or paid-leave status
- Does **not** accrue during unpaid leave (LWP) or long-term absence
- Is capped at **30 days** for carry-forward purposes (see §6.7)

**Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), and other leave types** are credited as a full annual balance on:

- January 1 for existing employees
- The date of confirmation for employees completing probation
- The date of joining for employees eligible on day 1 (SL, BL only)

### 6.4 Crediting Schedule

| Leave Type | Credit Date | Amount Credited |
|---|---|---|
| CL | January 1 | 12 days (or prorated for mid-year joiners) |
| SL | Date of joining (day 1) | 10 days prorated for the year |
| EL | 1st of each month | 1.25 days per month |
| ML / PL | On approval | Full entitlement (per instance) |
| BL | On approval | 5 days per qualifying event |

### 6.5 Prorating Rules

Employees joining **after January 1** receive prorated allowances computed on a monthly basis:

`Prorated Allowance = Annual Allowance × (Months Remaining / 12)`

Rounding: computed to the nearest **0.5 day**.

**Worked examples:**

| Employee | Joining Date | Leave Type | Months Remaining | Prorated Allowance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Employee A | 2026-04-01 | CL | 9 | 12 × 9/12 = **9 days** |
| Employee B | 2026-08-15 | CL | 4.5 | 12 × 4.5/12 = **4.5 days** |
| Employee C | 2026-10-01 | CL | 3 | 12 × 3/12 = **3 days** |
| Employee D | 2026-02-01 | SL | 11 | 10 × 11/12 = **9 days** |

Part-time employees (defined as <30 hours per week on a regular basis) receive prorated allowances at **50%** of the standard entitlement for CL, SL, EL, ML, and PL. Bereavement leave is not prorated.

### 6.6 Advance Leave

Employees may apply for leave **before** the entitlement has been fully accrued, subject to:

- Maximum advance: **5 days** per leave type per year
- Manager approval is mandatory for any advance
- Advance leave is deducted from future accruals
- If an employee exits the company with advance leave taken but not yet accrued, the unaccrued portion is recovered from the final settlement

### 6.7 Carry-Forward

Unused leave at the end of the leave year is handled as follows:

| Leave Type | Carry-Forward Rule |
|---|---|
| CL | Up to **5 days** may be carried forward to the next leave year |
| SL | **Not carried forward** — lapses on December 31 |
| EL | Up to **30 days** may be carried forward |
| ML, PL, BL | Not applicable — per-instance entitlements |

**Carry-forward mechanics:**

1. On December 31, unused CL beyond 5 days lapses
2. On January 1, carried-forward CL is added to the new year's CL credit
3. EL carry-forward is computed on December 31 and credited to the employee's EL balance on January 1
4. Employees may have up to 45 days of combined EL (current year accrual + carried forward) at any time

### 6.8 Lapse

Lapsed leave is forfeited and cannot be reinstated except under §16 (exceptions). Lapse events are:

- CL beyond 5 days on December 31
- All unused SL on December 31
- All unused CL on the employee's exit date (subject to final settlement)
- Any advance leave remaining unaccrued on the employee's exit date

### 6.9 Balance Visibility

Balances are updated in **real time** on the HR365 ESS portal under the "My Leave" tab. Balance is reflected:

- Immediately after a request is approved (deducted)
- Immediately after a cancellation is approved (restored)
- Monthly for EL accrual (credited on the 1st)

Employees are responsible for verifying their balance before applying.

## 7. Detailed Leave Type Rules

### 7.1 Casual Leave (CL)

**Eligibility:** Confirmed employees only.

**Annual allowance:** 12 days.

**Purpose:** Short, unplanned personal absences. Example uses: family events, personal errands, cultural/religious observances not otherwise covered.

**Characteristic rules:**

- CL is intended for absences of **1 to 3 days**
- Absences exceeding 3 days should be applied as EL (if available) or LWP
- CL cannot be combined with SL in the same absence period
- CL is not permitted during a notice period without HR approval
- CL may be applied with **1 working day** advance notice for planned absences

**Common mistakes:**

- Applying CL for a 5-day absence (should be EL or LWP)
- Using CL for a medical reason (should be SL)
- Applying CL without checking that sufficient balance remains

**Example applications:**

| Scenario | Correct Leave Type |
|---|---|
| 2-day family event | CL |
| 1-day personal errand | CL |
| 4-day wedding in another city | EL (if available) or LWP |
| 2-day medical rest | SL |
| Half-day for a child's school function | CL (0.5 day deduction) |

### 7.2 Sick Leave (SL)

**Eligibility:** All employees from their date of joining.

**Annual allowance:** 10 days.

**Purpose:** Absence due to illness, medical treatment, recovery from injury, or medical appointments.

**Characteristic rules:**

- SL is available from day 1 for all employees, including probationary employees
- For 1 to 2 day absences, the manager must be notified on the day of absence (see §11)
- For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, a medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR365 portal within 5 working days of return
- SL is **not carried forward** — unused SL lapses on December 31
- SL cannot be used for non-medical reasons; misuse may result in disciplinary action

**Medical certificate requirements** — see §12.

**Interaction with WFH:** If an employee is unwell but able to work, they may use the WFH check-in mechanism instead of SL, subject to manager approval and Remote Work Policy (HR365-POL-004).

**Example applications:**

| Scenario | Correct Treatment |
|---|---|
| 1-day fever, called in at 8 AM | SL, day-of notification |
| 3-day flu with doctor's note | SL, certificate uploaded within 5 days |
| 6-hour medical appointment | SL (0.5 day) or CL (employee choice) |
| Recovering from surgery (14 days) | SL (10 days) + EL or LWP for the remainder |

### 7.3 Earned Leave (EL)

**Eligibility:** Confirmed employees only.

**Annual allowance:** 15 days, accrued at 1.25 days per month.

**Purpose:** Planned, extended absence. Example uses: vacations, extended personal travel, planned family events.

**Characteristic rules:**

- EL is the only leave type that accrues monthly
- EL is credited on the 1st of each month
- Minimum duration per application: 1 day
- Maximum continuous EL: 15 days in a single application, unless approved by HR
- EL may be combined with CL to extend an absence (e.g., 3 EL + 2 CL = 5-day absence)
- EL cannot be combined with SL for the same absence period

**Accrual examples:**

| Month | EL Credited | Running Balance (starting 0) |
|---|---:|---:|
| January | 1.25 | 1.25 |
| February | 1.25 | 2.50 |
| March | 1.25 | 3.75 |
| April | 1.25 | 5.00 |
| May | 1.25 | 6.25 |
| June | 1.25 | 7.50 |
| July | 1.25 | 8.75 |
| August | 1.25 | 10.00 |
| September | 1.25 | 11.25 |
| October | 1.25 | 12.50 |
| November | 1.25 | 13.75 |
| December | 1.25 | 15.00 |

### 7.4 Maternity Leave (ML)

**Eligibility:** Confirmed female employees.

**Allowance:** 26 weeks per qualifying event.

**Purpose:** Absence related to pregnancy, childbirth, and post-natal care.

**Characteristic rules:**

- ML is available in addition to the standard leave types
- Total ML: 26 weeks (approximately 182 calendar days)
- Not more than 8 weeks may be taken before the expected date of delivery
- ML must be applied via the HR365 portal with a medical certificate confirming pregnancy and expected date
- ML cannot be denied once eligibility and documentation are verified
- During ML, Health Insurance continues per Benefits Policy (HR365-POL-006)
- Other benefits (meal allowance, internet reimbursement) are paused during ML and resume on return
- ML is not carried forward; unused ML lapses at the end of the instance

**Return-to-work:**

- Employees must notify HR at least **14 calendar days** before the intended return date
- A phased return (part-time for up to 4 weeks) may be requested
- Reasonable accommodations (e.g., nursing room access) will be provided

**Medical complications:**

- Extended ML beyond 26 weeks requires medical documentation and HR approval
- Any absence beyond 26 weeks is treated as EL, LWP, or extended ML as applicable

### 7.5 Paternity Leave (PL)

**Eligibility:** Confirmed employees.

**Allowance:** 10 days per qualifying event.

**Purpose:** Absence related to the birth or adoption of a child.

**Characteristic rules:**

- PL must be taken within **90 days** of the birth or adoption
- PL can be taken in continuous block or split into up to 2 segments
- PL is not carried forward
- PL applies equally to biological and adoptive parents
- Medical documentation (birth certificate, adoption papers) must be uploaded within 30 days

### 7.6 Bereavement Leave (BL)

**Eligibility:** All employees.

**Allowance:** 5 days per qualifying event.

**Qualifying events:**

- Death of spouse
- Death of child
- Death of parent or parent-in-law
- Death of sibling
- Death of grandparent
- Death of any immediate family member in the employee's household

**Characteristic rules:**

- BL is available from day 1, including during probation
- BL may be taken as needed within **30 days** of the qualifying event
- Extended absence beyond 5 days requires EL, LWP, or HR approval for additional BL
- Documentation: a copy of the death certificate or equivalent must be uploaded to the HR365 portal within 15 days
- BL is not carried forward

### 7.7 Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)

**Eligibility:** Confirmed employees.

**Purpose:** Compensation for work performed on a company holiday, weekend, or beyond standard hours with pre-approval.

**Rules for earning:**

- Comp-Off must be **pre-approved** by the manager before the extra work is performed
- Each pre-approved holiday/weekend shift earns **1 Comp-Off day** for a full day, or **0.5 Comp-Off day** for a half day
- Comp-Off is credited to the employee's balance within 5 working days of the extra work
- Comp-Off must be used within **60 days** of earning — it lapses after that
- Comp-Off cannot be combined with SL for the same absence
- Unused Comp-Off at exit is not encashed

**Comp-Off application:**

- Applied for via the HR365 portal using the "Comp-Off" leave type
- Subject to manager approval like any other leave

### 7.8 Leave Without Pay (LWP)

**Eligibility:** All employees.

**Allowance:** Unlimited, subject to approval.

**Purpose:** Extended absence when no paid leave is available or when the absence exceeds the allowance of the relevant paid leave type.

**Characteristic rules:**

- LWP must be approved by both the manager and HR
- LWP is unpaid — deductions apply to the corresponding payroll cycle
- During LWP, EL does not accrue (see §6.3)
- Health Insurance continues during LWP periods up to 30 days; beyond 30 days, HR review is required
- Other benefits are paused during LWP
- LWP cannot be used to circumvent notice-period or exit obligations
- LWP is subject to a maximum of **60 days** in a single continuous block unless approved by the HR Head

## 8. Leave Combinations and Restrictions

### 8.1 Permitted Combinations

| Combination | Permitted? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CL + EL | ✓ | Common for extended absence |
| CL + SL | ✗ | Cannot be combined for the same absence |
| EL + SL | ✗ | Cannot be combined for the same absence |
| SL + LWP | ✓ | When SL is exhausted |
| EL + LWP | ✓ | When EL is exhausted |
| ML + LWP | ✓ | Extension of ML |
| PL + CL | ✓ | Extend PL beyond 10 days |
| Comp-Off + CL | ✓ | If both approved |

### 8.2 Restrictions

- Leave cannot be applied for a period longer than 30 days without HR approval
- Leave cannot be applied retroactively beyond 5 working days (see §11.4)
- Leave during probation is limited to SL and BL only
- Leave during notice period requires HR approval in addition to manager approval
- Leave on the first and last working day of a pay cycle requires manager+HR approval

## 9. Application Process

### 9.1 Advance Notice Requirements

| Leave Type | Minimum Advance Notice |
|---|---|
| CL (planned) | 1 working day |
| CL (unplanned) | Same-day notification to manager |
| SL (1–2 days) | Same-day notification |
| SL (3+ days) | Same-day notification + certificate within 5 days |
| EL | 2 working days |
| ML | 30 days (or as soon as pregnancy is confirmed) |
| PL | 7 days (or as soon as birth/adoption is confirmed) |
| BL | Same-day or next working day |
| LWP | 15 days |
| Comp-Off | 2 working days |

### 9.2 Submission Method

All leave must be applied for via the **HR365 ESS portal**:

1. Navigate to "My Leave" → "Apply for Leave"
2. Select leave type
3. Enter start date and end date
4. Provide reason (mandatory field)
5. Attach supporting documents (for SL 3+ days, ML, PL, BL)
6. Submit — a notification is sent to the manager

**Applications submitted outside the portal** (email, chat, verbal) are not valid. HR does not process leave based on verbal requests alone.

### 9.3 Backdated Leave

Leave that has already commenced cannot be applied for as "planned." Backdated applications require:

- Same-day notification to the manager (before or during the absence)
- Submission via the portal within **5 working days** of returning
- Supporting documentation for SL, BL, or any extended absence

Backdated applications beyond 5 working days require HR approval.

### 9.4 Same-Day Notification

For unplanned absences, employees must notify the direct reporting manager:

- **Before 10:00 AM** on the day of absence (for full-day absences)
- **By the start of the missed period** (for partial-day absences)
- Via the manager's preferred channel (WhatsApp, Slack, email, phone)
- With an expected return date or a note that the return date is unknown

Notification to teammates is **not** a substitute for notifying the manager.

## 10. Approval Workflow

### 10.1 Approval Chain

| Request Type | First Approver | Second Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Standard leave (CL, SL, EL, Comp-Off) | Direct reporting manager | — |
| ML, PL | Direct reporting manager | HR |
| LWP | Direct reporting manager | HR |
| BL | Direct reporting manager | — (HR is notified) |
| Leave during probation | Direct reporting manager | HR |
| Leave during notice period | Direct reporting manager | HR |
| Leave > 30 days | Direct reporting manager | HR Head |

### 10.2 Response SLA

| Request Type | Manager SLA |
|---|---|
| SL (same-day) | Acknowledge within **2 hours** |
| CL | **1 working day** |
| EL | **2 working days** |
| ML / PL | **3 working days** |
| LWP | **3 working days** |
| Comp-Off | **2 working days** |

### 10.3 Auto-Escalation

If a manager does not action a leave request within the SLA, the request **auto-escalates** to the department head. Auto-escalation notifications are sent:

- 1 hour after the SLA elapses (reminder to manager)
- 4 hours after the SLA elapses (escalation notification to department head)
- 1 working day after the SLA elapses (escalation notification to HR)

### 10.4 Rejection

A manager may reject a leave request only with a **documented reason**, which must be one of:

- Business-critical absence coverage not available
- Insufficient leave balance
- Leave requested during a mandatory blackout period (see §14)
- Documentation missing or insufficient (for SL, ML, PL, BL)
- Policy violation (e.g., combining non-combinable leave types)

Manager rejections are visible to HR for audit.

### 10.5 Manager Cannot Reject

A manager may **not** reject leave:

- On the grounds that the employee "should have informed earlier" if the employee met the advance-notice requirement
- For SL with valid documentation
- For BL with valid documentation
- For ML (once eligibility is confirmed)
- Arbitrarily or without a documented reason

Employees may escalate rejected leave via §17.

## 11. Documentation Requirements

### 11.1 Notification vs Documentation

| Requirement | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Notification** | Informing the manager (before or on the day) — verbal or chat is fine |
| **Documentation** | Uploading a certificate or proof to the HR365 portal — mandatory for certain leave types |

### 11.2 Medical Certificates (Sick Leave)

Required for:

- SL of **3 or more consecutive days**
- Any SL where the manager requests verification

**Content requirements:**

- Issued by a **registered medical practitioner** (MBBS, BAMS, or equivalent)
- Must state: employee name, dates of absence, diagnosis category, and recommended rest period
- Must be on the practitioner's letterhead or include their registration number
- Must be uploaded within **5 working days** of return

**Acceptable diagnosis categories:**

- Fever-related illness
- Injury or accident
- Surgical recovery
- Mental health (depression, anxiety, burnout — employee is not required to disclose the specific diagnosis)
- Chronic condition flare-up
- Preventive care or medical procedure

Employees are **not** required to disclose specific diagnosis details beyond the category. HR treats medical information as confidential.

### 11.3 Maternity and Paternity Documentation

- **ML:** Medical certificate confirming pregnancy and expected date of delivery; birth certificate or hospital discharge summary after delivery
- **PL:** Birth certificate, hospital discharge summary, or adoption order
- All documentation must be uploaded within **30 days** of the qualifying event

### 11.4 Bereavement Documentation

- Copy of death certificate, obituary notice, or equivalent
- Uploaded within **15 days** of the qualifying event
- Only the immediate manager and HR see this documentation

### 11.5 Non-Compliance

Failure to upload required documentation within the stated period may result in:

- The leave being converted to LWP (unpaid)
- A payroll deduction for the absence
- Disciplinary action for repeated non-compliance

## 12. Cancellation and Modification

### 12.1 Cancelling Approved Leave

- **Up to 1 day before the leave start date:** Employee may cancel via the HR365 portal directly
- **After leave has started:** Requires manager approval and an HR note
- **Emergency cancellation:** Notify the manager immediately; submit the portal cancellation within 1 working day

### 12.2 Modifying Leave

- Modifications (change dates, change leave type) are treated as new requests
- The original leave must be cancelled first (if approved) and a new request submitted
- Modifications are subject to the same approval SLA as new requests

### 12.3 Truncating Leave (Early Return)

If an employee returns early from leave:

- The manager is notified as soon as the return date is known
- The HR365 portal is updated to reflect the actual return date
- The unused leave balance is restored within 2 working days

### 12.4 Extending Leave

If an employee needs to extend leave beyond the original end date:

- The extension must be submitted as a **new request** before the original end date
- The extension is subject to the same advance-notice requirements
- Extensions during unplanned absence (e.g., SL) may be applied for retroactively per §9.3

## 13. Balance and Payroll Interaction

### 13.1 Payroll Cutoff

Payroll processing for the month is locked on the **25th of each month** (see Payroll Policy, HR365-POL-007). Leave requests actioned **before the 25th** affect the current month's payroll. Requests actioned after the 25th apply to the following month's cycle.

### 13.2 Unpaid Leave Deductions

LWP is deducted at the daily gross rate:

`Daily Gross Rate = Monthly Gross Salary / Working Days in Month`

Deductions appear on the payslip under "Unpaid Leave Deduction" with the dates itemized.

### 13.3 Leave Encashment

- **EL encashment on exit:** Employees may encash up to 30 days of unused EL at their last drawn salary
- **EL encashment mid-employment:** Not permitted
- **CL encashment:** Not permitted
- **Comp-Off encashment:** Not permitted

### 13.4 Effect of Leave on Payroll Components

| Leave Type | Effect on Salary | Effect on EL Accrual |
|---|---|---|
| CL | None (paid) | None (accrues normally) |
| SL | None (paid) | None (accrues normally) |
| EL | None (paid) | None (accrues normally) |
| ML (up to 26 weeks) | None (paid) | **Paused** |
| PL | None (paid) | None |
| BL | None (paid) | None |
| Comp-Off | None (paid) | None |
| LWP | Deducted at daily gross rate | **Paused** |

## 14. Leave Blackout Periods

NimbusWorks observes the following blackout periods during which leave is restricted:

| Blackout Period | Reason | Exception Process |
|---|---|---|
| Last 5 working days of the financial quarter (Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec) | Financial close | HR Head approval |
| Last 3 working days of the fiscal year (March 27–31) | Year-end closing | HR Head approval |
| Major product launch windows (announced quarterly) | Business criticality | Department Head + HR Head approval |
| First 5 working days of a new hire's onboarding (for the hiring manager) | Onboarding support | HR approval |

Blackout restrictions do not apply to:

- SL (medical necessity)
- BL
- ML / PL
- Pre-approved EL submitted before the blackout was announced

Managers must publish blackout periods **at least 30 days in advance** via the HR365 portal.

## 15. Interaction with Other Policies

### 15.1 Attendance Policy (HR365-POL-002)

- Employees on approved leave are **not** marked absent during the leave period
- Leave is recorded in the attendance system automatically on approval
- Attendance corrections and leave requests are separate workflows

### 15.2 Remote Work Policy (HR365-POL-004)

- Employees on WFH are not "on leave"
- WFH check-in is required for WFH days; failure to check in without prior notice counts as unmarked absence
- Employees who are unwell may use SL instead of WFH if unable to work

### 15.3 Payroll Policy (HR365-POL-007)

- Leave balances affect payroll processing per §13
- EL encashment on exit is processed in the final settlement
- LWP deductions follow §13.2

### 15.4 Benefits Policy (HR365-POL-006)

- Health Insurance continues during ML, PL, and BL
- Other benefits may pause during ML and LWP per HR365-POL-006

### 15.5 Code of Conduct (HR365-POL-005)

- Misuse of leave (e.g., using SL for non-medical reasons, falsifying documentation) is a conduct violation and may result in disciplinary action

### 15.6 Company Notices (HR365-POL-011)

- Notices may announce temporary leave-related changes (e.g., extended holidays, blackout announcements)
- Notices override this policy only for the specific, time-bounded scope they declare

## 16. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require **HR Head approval** and are granted only under specific circumstances:

### 16.1 Permitted Exception Categories

- **Medical emergency** preventing standard documentation timelines
- **Extended medical leave** beyond standard SL/EL entitlement
- **Extended bereavement** following loss of a dependent
- **Family emergency** requiring urgent extended absence
- **Statutory obligation** (jury duty, legal proceedings, government service)
- **Company-mandated absence** (training, conference, offsite)

### 16.2 Exception Request Process

1. Employee submits an exception request via the HR365 portal with supporting documentation
2. Request includes: leave dates, reason, requested exception, and proposed alternative
3. HR reviews and either approves, rejects, or modifies
4. Response within **5 working days**
5. Approved exceptions are logged for audit

### 16.3 Exceptions Not Permitted

- Retroactive exceptions for leave taken more than 30 days ago without documentation
- Exceptions to override blackout periods for non-emergency reasons
- Exceptions that would cause the employee to exceed 45 days of EL balance
- Exceptions to combine non-combinable leave types

All disputes and their resolutions are logged in the HR365 case management system. The log is reviewed monthly by HR leadership.

## 19. Frequently Asked Questions

### Basic Leave Questions

**Q: How many total days of leave do I get per year?**
A: A confirmed employee receives 12 CL + 10 SL + 15 EL = 37 days per year. Maternity, paternity, and bereavement leaves are additional.

**Q: When can I start using leave?**
A: Sick Leave and Bereavement Leave are available from day 1. Casual, Earned, Maternity, and Paternity Leave are available after confirmation.

**Q: Where do I see my leave balance?**
A: On the HR365 ESS portal under "My Leave." Balances update in real time.

**Q: Can I apply for leave for someone else?**
A: No. Leave must be applied for by the employee themselves. Managers cannot apply on behalf of an employee.

**Q: What happens if I don't have enough balance?**
A: You can apply for advance leave (up to 5 days per type per year) with manager approval, or use LWP.

### Casual Leave

**Q: What's the maximum CL I can take at once?**
A: 3 consecutive days. Beyond that, use EL or LWP.

**Q: Can I combine CL and SL?**
A: No. Not for the same absence period.

**Q: Does CL carry forward?**
A: Up to 5 days. Beyond that, it lapses on December 31.

**Q: Can I take CL during probation?**
A: No. Only SL and BL are available during probation.

**Q: Can I take half-day CL?**
A: Yes, with manager approval. Two half-days = 1 full CL day.

### Sick Leave

**Q: Do I need a doctor's note for 1 day of SL?**
A: No. Same-day notification to your manager is sufficient.

**Q: What about 3 days of SL?**
A: You need a medical certificate uploaded within 5 working days of returning.

**Q: Can I use SL for a family member's illness?**
A: No. SL is for the employee's own medical reasons. For a family member, use CL or EL.

**Q: What if I can't get a medical certificate?**
A: Notify HR immediately. Alternative documentation (pharmacy receipt, telemedicine record) may be accepted at HR's discretion.

**Q: Can I use SL for mental health days?**
A: Yes. Mental health is treated the same as physical health. You are not required to disclose the specific diagnosis.

**Q: What if I get sick while on leave?**
A: Notify your manager. The leave may be reclassified as SL if documentation is provided and the balance is available.

**Q: Is SL prorated if I join mid-year?**
A: Yes. See §6.5.

**Q: Does SL accrue?**
A: No. SL is credited as a full annual allowance at the start of the year (or on joining).

**Q: Can SL be encashed on exit?**
A: No.

### Earned Leave

**Q: How does EL accrue?**
A: 1.25 days per month, credited on the 1st of each month, provided you have at least 15 days of present or paid-leave status that month.

**Q: What's the maximum EL I can hold at once?**
A: 45 days (15 current year + 30 carried forward).

**Q: What happens to unused EL at year-end?**
A: Up to 30 days is carried forward. Beyond that, it lapses.

**Q: Can I take EL during probation?**
A: No. EL is available only after confirmation.

**Q: Can I encash EL without exiting?**
A: No. Only on exit is encashment allowed.

**Q: How is EL encashment calculated?**
A: Up to 30 days at your last drawn basic salary rate. See Payroll Policy (HR365-POL-007) for details.

**Q: Can I take more EL than my balance?**
A: No, except via advance leave (max 5 days per type per year, with manager approval).

### Maternity Leave

**Q: When can I start my ML?**
A: Up to 8 weeks before the expected date of delivery.

**Q: Do I need to inform HR in advance?**
A: Yes. Notify HR at least 30 days before the intended start date, or as soon as pregnancy is confirmed.

**Q: What if I have complications and need to extend?**
A: Extension requires medical documentation and HR approval. It may be treated as extended ML, EL, or LWP depending on the circumstances.

**Q: What happens to my EL accrual during ML?**
A: EL accrual pauses during ML.

**Q: Do benefits continue during ML?**
A: Health Insurance continues. Other benefits pause and resume on return.

**Q: Can I return to work part-time after ML?**
A: Yes. A phased return (part-time up to 4 weeks) is available on request.

### Paternity Leave

**Q: How many days do I get?**
A: 10 days.

**Q: When must I take it?**
A: Within 90 days of the birth or adoption.

**Q: Can I split PL into two blocks?**
A: Yes, up to 2 segments.

**Q: Does PL apply to adoption?**
A: Yes. Same 10-day entitlement.

### Bereavement Leave

**Q: Which relationships qualify?**
A: Spouse, child, parent, parent-in-law, sibling, grandparent, or any immediate household member.

**Q: How many days do I get per event?**
A: 5 days.

**Q: Do I need to provide a death certificate?**
A: Yes, within 15 days of the event. HR treats this documentation as confidential.

**Q: Can I extend BL?**
A: Yes, using EL or LWP with HR approval.

### Application and Approval

**Q: What's the minimum advance notice for planned leave?**
A: 2 working days for EL, 1 working day for CL, same-day for SL.

**Q: Can my manager reject my leave?**
A: Only with a documented reason per §10.4.

**Q: What if my manager doesn't respond?**
A: The request auto-escalates to the department head after the SLA.

**Q: Can I apply for leave on the same day?**
A: Yes, for SL and BL. For CL, only with manager approval.

**Q: Can I apply retroactively?**
A: Within 5 working days of returning. Beyond that, HR approval is needed.

**Q: How do I modify an approved leave?**
A: Cancel the original and submit a new request.

**Q: Can I cancel approved leave?**
A: Yes, up to 1 day before the leave start date via the portal. After starting, manager approval is required.

**Q: What if I need to extend my leave?**
A: Submit an extension request before the original end date.

**Q: What if I return early?**
A: Notify your manager. The unused balance is restored within 2 working days.

### Payroll and Balance

**Q: When does my payslip reflect a leave deduction?**
A: In the next payroll cycle if the LWP is actioned before the 25th.

**Q: Can I take leave without impacting my salary?**
A: Yes, if you have sufficient CL, SL, or EL balance. LWP impacts pay.

**Q: How is LWP deducted?**
A: At the daily gross rate (monthly gross / working days in the month).

**Q: Can I encash SL or CL?**
A: No.

**Q: What if there's a payroll error related to leave?**
A: Report it within 7 working days to the Finance Helpdesk.

### Blackout Periods

**Q: What are blackout periods?**
A: Periods when leave is restricted due to business-critical work. See §14.

**Q: Can I take leave during a blackout?**
A: Only with HR Head approval (except for SL, BL, ML, PL).

**Q: How much notice is given for a blackout?**
A: At least 30 days in advance, published on the HR365 portal.

### Exceptions and Escalation

**Q: Can I get an exception to the policy?**
A: Yes, in specific categories per §16. HR Head approval is required.

**Q: How do I escalate a rejected leave request?**
A: Contact the HR Helpdesk. If unresolved, it goes to the HR Head.

**Q: What's the response SLA for escalations?**
A: 5 working days.

## 20. Worked Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Planned Vacation

**Employee:** Confirmed, 8 years of service, balance = 10 CL, 5 SL, 12 EL.

**Request:** 7-day vacation starting in 3 weeks.

**Correct approach:**
- Apply via the HR365 portal with 2 working days' advance notice
- Select EL as the leave type (7 days is beyond CL's typical 1-3 day window)
- Attach no documentation (EL doesn't require it)
- Manager actions within 2 working days
- Balance after approval: 5 CL, 5 SL, 5 EL

### Scenario 2: Sudden Illness

**Employee:** Probationary, day 45.

**Event:** Wakes up with fever, cannot work.

**Correct approach:**
- Notify manager before 10:00 AM
- Same-day application via the portal under SL
- No documentation needed for 1 day
- SL balance prorated: 10 × ~10/12 ≈ 8 days available

### Scenario 3: Extended Illness

**Employee:** Confirmed, 3 years.

**Event:** Medical procedure requires 12 days of recovery.

**Correct approach:**
- Notify manager on day 1
- Apply for SL (10 days) + LWP (2 days) — SL is 10 days, so 2 days exceed the balance
- Upload medical certificate within 5 working days of return
- SL paid at full salary; LWP days deducted at daily gross rate

### Scenario 4: Family Emergency Abroad

**Employee:** Confirmed, 2 years.

**Event:** Father hospitalized overseas; needs 10 days.

**Correct approach:**
- Notify manager immediately (before or on day 1)
- Apply for BL (5 days — if the qualifying event is death; if just hospitalization, use EL or CL)
- For hospitalization without death: use EL (if available) + LWP
- Documentation: medical report or hospitalization proof uploaded within 15 days

### Scenario 5: Maternity

**Employee:** Confirmed female, 4 years.

**Event:** Pregnant, expected date of delivery in 5 months.

**Correct approach:**
- Notify HR as soon as pregnancy is confirmed
- Apply for ML 30 days before the intended start date
- Attach medical certificate confirming pregnancy and expected date
- ML: 26 weeks from start date
- Health Insurance continues; meal allowance and internet reimbursement pause
- Return-to-work notice given 14 days before return

### Scenario 6: Blackout Conflict

**Employee:** Confirmed, 1 year.

**Event:** Wants 5 days off during March 27–31 (financial year-end blackout).

**Correct approach:**
- Apply via the portal; the request will be flagged as conflicting with a blackout period
- Manager + HR Head approval required
- If not approved, the employee must reschedule or use SL if medical

### Scenario 7: Cancellation After Starting

**Employee:** Confirmed, 5 years.

**Event:** On day 3 of an approved 10-day EL; needs to return early due to a work emergency.

**Correct approach:**
- Notify the manager immediately
- Submit a cancellation for the remaining 7 days via the portal
- Manager approval + HR note
- Unused balance restored within 2 working days
- Payroll reflects the actual leave taken

### Scenario 8: Advance Leave

**Employee:** Confirmed, 6 months.

**Request:** Wants 3 days of CL in October; balance is only 1 day.

**Correct approach:**
- Apply for 1 day CL + 2 days advance CL (within the 5-day per type per year limit)
- Manager approval mandatory for the advance portion
- Advance CL is deducted from future accruals
- If employee exits before accrual, the unaccrued portion is recovered from final settlement

### Scenario 9: Comp-Off Usage

**Employee:** Confirmed, 2 years.

**Event:** Worked a Saturday with pre-approval for a project launch.

**Correct approach:**
- Comp-Off credited within 5 working days of the work
- Apply for Comp-Off day within 60 days
- Cannot combine Comp-Off with SL for the same absence
- Unused Comp-Off lapses after 60 days; no encashment

### Scenario 10: Bereavement

**Employee:** Probationary, day 20.

**Event:** Grandfather passed away.

**Correct approach:**
- Notify manager same day
- Apply for BL (5 days)
- Upload death certificate within 15 days
- BL is available during probation
- BL is fully paid and doesn't affect EL accrual

### Scenario 11: Documentation Failure

**Employee:** Confirmed, 1 year.

**Event:** Took 4 days of SL but didn't upload a medical certificate within 5 working days.

**Consequence:**
- The 4 days are reclassified as LWP
- Deduction applied in the next payroll cycle
- Repeated non-compliance may result in disciplinary action

### Scenario 12: Manager Doesn't Respond

**Employee:** Confirmed, 3 years.

**Event:** Applied for 3 days of EL; manager doesn't respond within 2 working days.

**Auto-escalation:**
- Request escalates to department head after the SLA
- Department head actions within 2 working days
- If still unactioned, HR intervenes
- Employee continues to be informed of escalation status via the portal

## Purpose
This policy defines NimbusWorks' rules for employee leave, including types, eligibility, and the application process.

## Scope
Applies to all confirmed and probationary employees; entitlements during probation are prorated as noted below.

## Policy

### 1. Leave Types

| Leave Type | Eligibility | Annual Allowance | Carry Forward |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Casual Leave (CL) | All employees, after probation | 12 days | Up to 5 days to next year |
| Sick Leave (SL) | All employees, from date of joining | 10 days | Not carried forward |
| Earned Leave (EL) | Confirmed employees only | 15 days (accrued at 1.25/month) | Up to 30 days |
| Maternity Leave | Confirmed female employees | 26 weeks | Not applicable |
| Paternity Leave | Confirmed employees | 10 days | Not applicable |
| Bereavement Leave | All employees | 5 days per event | Not applicable |

### 2. Eligibility
Probationary employees may take Sick Leave from day one but Casual and Earned Leave only after successful confirmation.

### 3. Leave Balance, Accrual, and Carry-Forward
- Earned Leave accrues monthly at 1.25 days and is credited on the 1st of each month.
- Unused Casual Leave beyond 5 days lapses at year-end (December 31).
- Leave balances are visible in real time on the HR365 Employee Self-Service portal.

### 4. Application Process
- Leave must be applied for through the HR365 portal at least 2 working days in advance for planned leave.
- Sick Leave of 1–2 days may be applied on the day of absence with same-day notification to the manager.
- Sick Leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate uploaded within 5 working days of return.

### 5. Approval Workflow
- The direct reporting manager is the first-level approver.
- Leave requests must be actioned (approved/rejected) within 2 working days; unactioned requests auto-escalate to the department head.

### 6. Cancellation
- Approved leave can be cancelled by the employee via the portal up to 1 day before the leave start date.
- Cancellations after leave has started require manager approval and an HR note.

### 7. Medical-Leave Documentation
Medical certificates must be issued by a registered medical practitioner and include diagnosis category, dates, and recommended rest period.

## Exceptions
Leave taken during a notice period requires additional approval from HR in addition to the manager.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: apply in advance and submit required documentation.
- Managers: action requests within SLA.
- HR Department: maintain accurate leave ledgers and audit balances quarterly.

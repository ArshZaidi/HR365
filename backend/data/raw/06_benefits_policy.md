## Purpose
Describes the benefits available to NimbusWorks employees and the process for claiming and reimbursing eligible expenses.

## Scope
Applies to all confirmed employees; probationary employees have access to Health Insurance from day one, other benefits after confirmation.

## Policy

### 1. Benefits Overview
NimbusWorks provides: Group Health Insurance, Life Insurance, Meal Allowance, Internet Reimbursement, Learning & Development Allowance, and an Employee Assistance Program (EAP).

### 2. Eligibility
- Health Insurance: all employees, from date of joining.
- Life Insurance, Meal Allowance, Internet Reimbursement, L&D Allowance: confirmed employees only.
- EAP: all employees and their immediate family members, from date of joining.

### 3. Insurance / Health Benefits
- Group Health Insurance covers the employee and up to 4 dependents, with a sum insured of ₹5,00,000 per family per year.
- Enrollment/updates to dependents must be completed within 30 days of joining or of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, etc.).

### 4. Reimbursements
- Internet Reimbursement: up to ₹1,000/month against a submitted bill.
- L&D Allowance: up to ₹20,000/year for approved courses, certifications, or conferences relevant to the employee's role.
- Meal Allowance: ₹150/working day, credited monthly, no bill required.

### 5. Claim Timelines
- Reimbursement claims must be submitted via the HR365 Finance portal within 60 days of the expense date.
- Approved claims are processed in the next payroll cycle after approval.
- Claims submitted after 60 days are not eligible for reimbursement except with Finance Head approval.

### 6. Exclusions
- Cosmetic procedures are excluded from Health Insurance coverage unless medically necessary.
- L&D claims for courses unrelated to the employee's current or planned role are not eligible.
- Personal entertainment or non-work travel expenses are never reimbursable.

## Exceptions
Employees on approved long-term leave (e.g., maternity leave) continue to receive Health Insurance coverage; other benefits are paused and resume on return.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: submit claims with valid documentation within the claim window.
- Managers: approve L&D claims for role relevance.
- Finance & HR: process approved claims within the stated payroll cycle.

## Contact / Escalation
Benefits queries: benefits@nimbusworks.example. Claim disputes: escalate to Finance Head via finance-escalation@nimbusworks.example.

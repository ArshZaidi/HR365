## 1. Purpose

Describes the benefits available to NimbusWorks employees and the process for claiming and reimbursing eligible expenses.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all confirmed employees. Probationary employees have access to Health Insurance from day 1; other benefits activate on confirmation.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |

| Sum Insured | Maximum amount payable by the insurer per policy period |
| Dependent | Spouse, children, parents, or parents-in-law as defined by the insurer |

## 4. Benefits Overview

NimbusWorks provides:

| Benefit | Eligibility | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Group Health Insurance | All employees from day 1 | Annual policy |
| Life Insurance | Confirmed employees | Annual policy |
| Meal Allowance | Confirmed employees | Monthly credit |
| Internet Reimbursement | Confirmed employees | Monthly (against bill) |
| Learning & Development Allowance | Confirmed employees | Annual (up to ₹20,000) |
| Employee Assistance Program | All employees + immediate family, from day 1 | 24×7 access |

## 5. Group Health Insurance

- **Sum insured:** ₹5,00,000 per family per year
- **Coverage:** Employee + up to 4 dependents
- **Network:** Cashless at empanelled hospitals; reimbursement at non-network hospitals
- **Waiting periods:** Standard waiting period per insurer for pre-existing conditions
- **Pre-authorization:** Required for planned hospitalization; emergency admissions must be intimated within 24 hours
- **Exclusions:** Cosmetic procedures unless medically necessary; non-medical items; experimental treatments

**Enrollment windows:**

- **On joining:** Within 30 days of date of joining
- **Qualifying life event:** Within 30 days of marriage, birth, adoption, etc.
- **Annual window:** Announced annually by HR — typically in January

**Dependents:**

- Spouse
- Up to 2 children (biological or adopted)
- Parents or parents-in-law (either set, not both)

## 6. Life Insurance

- **Coverage:** As defined in the annual policy summary (visible on the HR365 portal)
- **Nominee:** Employee must declare a nominee at enrollment; nominee changes must be submitted in writing
- **Claim:** Family may claim by contacting HR and the insurer directly

## 7. Meal Allowance

- **Amount:** ₹150 per working day
- **Credited:** Monthly, along with payroll
- **No bill required**
- Not applicable during leave days exceeding 30 consecutive days

## 8. Internet Reimbursement

- **Limit:** Up to ₹1,000 per month
- **Requirement:** Must submit an internet bill (broadband or mobile)
- **Submission:** Via the HR365 Finance portal
- **Approval:** Manager verifies the employee is on remote/hybrid arrangement
- **Payment:** Credited in the next payroll cycle after approval

## 9. Learning & Development Allowance

- **Limit:** Up to ₹20,000 per year
- **Coverage:** Courses, certifications, conferences, and workshops relevant to the employee's current or planned role
- **Approval:** Manager confirms role relevance; HR processes claim
- **Submission:** Via the HR365 Finance portal with the invoice or receipt
- **Timeline:** Claim must be submitted within 60 days of the course completion

**Not covered:**

- Courses unrelated to the employee's role
- Personal interest courses
- Repeat certification for the same topic (unless renewal is required)
- Travel and lodging for conferences (submitted separately as business travel)

## 10. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

- Free, confidential counseling and support
- Available 24×7
- Covers employee + immediate family (spouse, children, parents)
- Unlimited sessions per topic (fair-use limits may apply)
- Use is entirely voluntary and confidential
- Contact details on the HR365 portal

## 11. Reimbursement Process

### 11.1 Submission

1. Navigate to the HR365 Finance portal
2. Select claim type (Internet, L&D, Meal top-up, etc.)
3. Enter amount, date of expense, and purpose
4. Attach valid documentation
5. Submit — manager is notified

### 11.2 Claim Window

- Must be submitted within **60 days** of the expense date
- Claims submitted after 60 days are not eligible except with Finance Head approval
- Claims for the previous calendar year must be submitted by January 31

### 11.3 Approval

- Manager approves (for L&D: confirms role relevance)
- HR reviews for policy compliance
- Finance processes the payment

### 11.4 Payment

- Approved claims are processed in the **next payroll cycle** after approval
- Timing may differ if approval occurs after the payroll cutoff (25th of the month)
- Claims processed through payroll are shown as a separate line item on the payslip

## 12. Exclusions

- Cosmetic procedures (unless medically necessary)
- L&D courses unrelated to the employee's role
- Personal entertainment
- Non-work travel
- Claims without valid documentation
- Claims beyond the submission window (except with Finance Head approval)
- Duplicate claims

## 13. Long-Term Leave Interactions

- **Maternity Leave:** Health Insurance continues. Other benefits (Meal, Internet) are paused and resume on return.
- **Paternity Leave:** Benefits continue uninterrupted (leave ≤ 10 days).
- **LWP ≤ 30 days:** Health Insurance continues; other benefits may continue based on payroll.
- **LWP > 30 days:** Health Insurance may require premium continuation; HR review required.

## 14. Tax Implications

- Meal Allowance is a cash allowance and is taxable per applicable tax rules
- Internet Reimbursement is a reimbursement against actual expenses — not taxable if supported by bills
- L&D reimbursement is taxable if not related to role-specific skill upgrade
- Insurance premiums borne by the Company are not taxable in the employee's hands

Employees should consult a tax advisor for specifics.

## 15. Responsibilities

**Employee:** Submit claims within the claim window with valid documentation.
**Manager:** Approve L&D claims for role relevance; verify WFH status for Internet.
**HR:** Review claims for policy compliance; process approved claims.
**Finance:** Process approved claims within the stated payroll cycle.

## Purpose
Defines the rules employees must follow to protect NimbusWorks accounts, devices, and data.

## Scope
Applies to all employees using company-provided or company-connected systems, whether on-site or remote.

## Policy

### 1. Accounts
- Each employee is issued a single company email/account on joining, deactivated within 24 hours of exit.
- Account sharing between employees is strictly prohibited.

### 2. Passwords / MFA
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters, changed every 90 days, and not reused across the last 5 passwords.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, and all core business systems.

### 3. Company Devices
- Company laptops must have disk encryption and endpoint protection software active at all times.
- Installation of unapproved software requires IT approval via the HR365 IT Request module.

### 4. VPN
- VPN is mandatory for remote access to internal systems (see Remote Work Policy, HR365-POL-004).
- VPN sessions auto-disconnect after 8 hours of inactivity and must be re-authenticated.

### 5. Data Handling
- Confidential company or client data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage.
- Data must be shared only via approved company tools (company email, approved file-sharing platforms).

### 6. Security Incident Reporting
- Lost/stolen devices must be reported to IT within 2 hours via it-security@nimbusworks.example or the 24x7 IT hotline.
- Suspicious emails (phishing) should be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in the company email client; do not click links or reply.
- All security incidents are logged and reviewed by IT Security within 24 hours.

## Exceptions
Contractors requiring system access must have a time-bound account (maximum 90 days, renewable) approved by IT and the sponsoring manager.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: follow password/MFA rules and report incidents promptly.
- IT Department: provision/deprovision accounts and monitor security incidents.
- Managers: sponsor and review contractor access.

## Contact / Escalation
IT Security: it-security@nimbusworks.example. Lost device / urgent incidents: 24x7 IT hotline (see HR365 portal). Escalation: Head of IT.
